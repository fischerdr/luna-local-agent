# Vibe City: Controls and Camera
Audience: player
Keywords: controls, mouse, keyboard, hotkeys, shortcuts, pan, scroll, zoom, mouse wheel, rotate, camera, WASD, arrow keys, Escape, view modes, top bar, HUD, tooltips, inspector, tile info, double click, drag to build, build menu, toolbar, cursor
Summary: How to move around and operate Vibe City with the mouse and keyboard: panning, zooming, rotating, every hotkey, the toolbar and its drawers, and dragging to build with a price preview. It also explains the top bar readouts, the view modes button, tooltips and the tile inspector.

## Mouse controls
The mouse in Vibe City does most of the work. With the Pan and Inspect tool (the top toolbar button), dragging with the left button moves the map. Dragging with the right or middle button always pans, whatever tool you hold. With a building tool, a left click builds on the tile under the pointer and a left drag builds or marks out a plan; the right button cancels a plan being dragged.

A press counts as a click if the pointer moves no more than 5 pixels before release. With the Pan tool, a single click opens buildings you can go into: an Art Gallery, Park, Casino, Club, Track, Arcade, Rail Depot (the ticket office), School or University (the classroom). A double click on the same tile within 0.4 seconds opens the tile inspector.

## Keyboard shortcuts
The keyboard shortcuts in Vibe City are:

- W, A, S, D or the arrow keys: pan the map up, left, down and right while held. Panning moves at the same screen speed at any zoom.
- Q: rotate the view left by 90 degrees.
- E: rotate the view right by 90 degrees.
- V: cycle the map view (City view, Pollution map, Crime map, Utilities).
- B: hide or show the build menu (toolbar).
- F11: toggle full screen.
- Escape: cancel a build plan, close menus, drawers and most windows, or get off a train ride.
- Enter: confirms the seed box in the New City window and the city name box.

The map shortcuts are off while you type in a text box or while a viewer window is open (the Art Gallery, a Park, the Casino, the Club, the Track or the Station). In the desktop app, Ctrl+R reloads the game and throws away an unsaved city. There is no shortcut for pause or game speed.

## Zooming
Zoom in Vibe City is on the mouse wheel: roll up to zoom in, down to zoom out. The zoom steps through fixed levels: 0.25x, 0.35x, 0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x, 2.5x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x and 8x. One wheel notch moves one level; a fast flick can move up to three. The zoom centres on the point under the mouse pointer, so you can zoom straight into the spot you are looking at. While the wheel turns, a badge in the middle of the map reads, for example, "Zoom 1.25x" and fades a moment after you stop.

Some details only appear close up: water ripples and ground textures from about 1.1x, cars as vehicles rather than dots from 1.2x, and fine vehicle details from 2.2x.

## Rotating the camera
The camera in Vibe City can face four directions, in 90 degree steps. Use the Rotate Left and Rotate Right buttons in the top bar, or press Q and E. Rotating keeps your zoom level and the spot in the middle of the screen, so the place you were looking at stays centred. The region map in the Neighbours window turns to match. Everything is redrawn from the new angle; nothing on the map changes. Rotating helps you see and click tiles hidden behind tall buildings or hills.

## The view modes button
The View button in Vibe City's top bar switches what the map shows, cycling in order through four views. The V key does the same.

- City view: the normal city.
- Pollution map: how dirty the air is, from dark (clean) through yellow and orange to red (filthy).
- Crime map: how bad crime is, from dark (safe) to red.
- Utilities: a survey drawing of the water pipes, power lines, tunnel bores, pumps, towers and generators. Bright magenta tiles want water and have no main in reach.

Outside City view the button lights up. In the Utilities view only the Water Pipe, Power Line, Tunnel, Bulldoze and Pan tools work; the rest are greyed out. Picking the Water Pipe tool switches to Utilities for you and switches back when you pick another tool.

## The top bar
The top bar in Vibe City runs across the top of the screen. From left to right:

- City name: click it to rename the city.
- Funds: the treasury, shown in red below zero.
- Pop: the number of residents.
- Date: month and year, starting at Jan 1.
- R, C, I meter: Residential, Commercial and Industrial demand. Above the middle line the city wants more of that zone, below it less. Click it for the Demand Report: a two year graph and a list of what the city needs.
- Radio, View (cycles the map view), City Stats (budget, tax, power, water, transport, mood) and Newspaper.
- Rotate Left and Rotate Right.
- Pause, Normal and Fast.
- The main menu button.

## Tooltips
Tooltips in Vibe City appear when you rest the mouse on a toolbar or top bar button, with a title and a short description. Toolbar tooltips give the tool's price, upkeep, size and placement rule, for example "Rail Depot: $500. Goes beside track on level ground. A line needs two depots to run." They are the quickest way to check what a building costs, how far it reaches and what it needs. A tooltip hides as soon as you click.

## The toolbar and tool drawers
The toolbar in Vibe City runs down the left side of the screen: Pan and Inspect at the top, then seven categories: Zoning, Transport, Utilities, Civic, Decor, Land and Clear. Clicking a category picks the tool you last used from it and opens a drawer of its tools. Picking a tool, clicking elsewhere or pressing Escape closes the drawer. Every tool in a drawer wears its price, such as "$50/tile", "$1,500" or "$25/step", and tools you cannot afford are dimmed.

The tab on the edge of the toolbar slides the build menu away for more map, and back. The B key does the same, and the choice is remembered.

## The cursor
The cursor in Vibe City is an outline around the tile under the mouse, with a small floating badge showing the picture of the tool you are holding. The outline is teal when the tool would work, a lighter red when holding Bulldoze, and a deep red when the tool cannot be used on that tile (for example a building that is not allowed there, a road over a built-up lot, or a tool that does not work in the current view). For buildings bigger than one tile, the outline covers the whole 2 by 2 or 3 by 3 square. With Raise, Lower or Level, the badge also shows the ground height under the cursor, such as "Height 1 of 3" or "Under water".

## Dragging to build
Dragging to build in Vibe City shows you the price before you spend anything. With Residential, Commercial, Industrial or Bulldoze, a drag marks out a rectangle from corner to corner. With Road, Rail, Power Line, Water Pipe or Tunnel, a drag marks a straight line one tile wide along whichever direction you dragged furthest; to make a bend, release and drag again from the corner.

While you drag, tiles that will change are washed in colour, tiles the tool cannot touch are shown faintly, and a price tag over the end of the drag shows the exact total, including bridges and hill surcharges. If you cannot afford it, the selection and price turn red.

Nothing is built or paid for until you let go of the button. To call it off, press the right mouse button or Escape, or pan away. If the money runs out partway through, the plan builds as far as the money goes and stops.

All other tools, such as single buildings, parks, trees and the land tools, build on each tile as the pointer crosses it while the button is held.

## The tile inspector
The tile inspector in Vibe City opens when you double click a tile with the Pan tool. It is a small panel titled with what is on the tile, kept up to date while open. Depending on the tile it shows coordinates and elevation, road class, a zone's level (such as "2 / 4"), road access, power, water and draw, coverage radius, a generator's output, a depot's status, freight, pollution and crime, and any tunnel, water main, power line or level crossing on the tile.

## Common questions
**Q: How do I move the map in Vibe City?**
A: Drag with the left mouse button while the Pan tool is selected, drag with the right or middle button with any tool, or hold W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.

**Q: How do I rotate the view?**
A: Press Q to rotate left or E to rotate right, or use the two rotate buttons in the top bar. Each press turns the view 90 degrees and keeps your zoom and position.

**Q: How do I zoom in and out?**
A: Use the mouse wheel. It steps through fixed levels from 0.25x to 8x and zooms toward the mouse pointer.

**Q: How do I see information about a tile?**
A: Select the Pan tool and double click the tile. The inspector panel opens with its details.

**Q: How do I cancel a road I am dragging?**
A: Press the right mouse button or Escape before letting go. Nothing is spent until you release the left button.

**Q: Why is my cursor red?**
A: The tool cannot be used on that tile, for example a building that needs clear level ground, a road over a built-up lot, or a surface tool used in the Utilities view.

**Q: How do I hide the toolbar?**
A: Press B, or click the tab on the edge of the build menu. Press B again to bring it back.

**Q: What do the R, C and I bars mean?**
A: They show demand for residential, commercial and industrial zones. Above the middle line the city wants more; below it the city wants less. Click them for a two year graph and advice.
