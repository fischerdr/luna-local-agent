# Vibe City: The Race Track and Betting on Horses
Audience: player
Keywords: track, race track, racecourse, horse racing, horses, races, betting, bet, odds, fractional odds, evens, favourite, long shot, bookie, bookmaker, payout, returns, stake, win bet, Vibe Downs, Lavender Park, Kitty Meadows, Solid Gold Downs
Summary: The Track is a $10,000 three by three venue in Vibe City that boosts growth within 8 tiles and gives 24 jobs. Click it with the pan tool to bet the city's money on five horse races with a bookie. A winning bet returns the stake times the odds plus the stake.

## The Track building
In Vibe City the Track is in the Decor drawer of the build menu. It takes a 3 by 3 lot, and the whole square has to be clear, dry, level ground at one height (trees are fine; they come down). It costs $10,000 (1.6 times that on high hill ground) and $50 a month upkeep. It needs power and water: 3 MW and 3 water units per tile, 27 of each for the whole lot. An open Track:
- boosts growth for zoned lots within 8 tiles, measured from each of its tiles,
- gives 24 jobs,
- makes no crime: a day at the races is not a night out.
A shut Track gives nothing. Each Track gets a name by where it stands: Vibe Downs, Lavender Park, Kitty Meadows or Solid Gold Downs.

## Going to the races
In Vibe City, choose the pan tool and click any tile of a Track. The race window opens with the track's name, a bookie on the stand, the race card and the course. A bugle sounds the call to the post once for each new race.

Each race has five horses, one per stall, in red, blue, green, gold and purple silks. Their names come from a list of 24 (Lavender Lightning, Vibe Check, Neon Nights, Midnight Mayor, Tax Break, Whisker Wonder, Long Shot, Sure Thing and more), and no name appears twice in one race. Each horse shows its price (odds) on the card.

## How to place a bet
Betting at the Vibe City Track is a straight win bet: you back one horse to come first.
1. Pick a horse by clicking it on the card, or press 1 to 5 for stalls 1 to 5.
2. Add chips: $100, $500, $1K, $5K or $25K. Clear starts over. The bet is between $100 and $50,000, and never more than the city has.
3. The bookie's slip shows your stake, the horse, its odds and what it returns.
4. Press They're Off (or Enter or Space) to start the race.
The stake leaves the treasury when they are off. After the race press Next race (or Enter or Space) for a new card. Your last stake is ready again on the next race if the city can cover it.

## Odds and payouts
The Vibe City bookie prices horses in traditional fractional odds, from shortest to longest: 1/2, 4/5, Evens, 6/4, 2/1, 5/2, 3/1, 4/1, 5/1, 6/1, 8/1, 10/1, 12/1, 14/1, 16/1, 20/1, 25/1 and 33/1.

A winning bet returns your stake times the odds, plus your stake back. For example:
- $1,000 at 5/2 returns $3,500 (a profit of $2,500).
- $1,000 at Evens returns $2,000.
- $500 at 10/1 returns $5,500.
If your horse does not win, you lose the stake. There are no place or each way bets: second is as good as last.

The shortest priced horse is the favourite. A price of 8/1 or longer is a long shot. Across all horses and races the book pays back about 87 cents on the dollar, less than blackjack or poker at the casino.

## How races are decided
In Vibe City every horse has a hidden form for each race, rolled fresh every time and never shown. The odds are set from that form, so a shorter price really does mean a better chance, but the bookie trims every price a little and rounds it down the ladder; that is the house's edge.

When they are off, the finishing order is drawn from the horses' true chances: the winner first, then second from those left, and so on. The running you watch takes about 14 seconds and is only a picture of that result. Horses drift about on the way and the lead can change, but nothing about how a horse looks mid race tells you who will win. The crowd cheers when the winner's nose hits the post.

If you close the window during a race, the race still counts: the result was already drawn, so the book pays or keeps your stake.

## The bookies
The Vibe City Track has two bookies, the brown bookie and the blond bookie, as the game's pictures call them. One of them is on the stand each time you open a Track, never the one from last time. The bookie looks sly while he writes your slip, shocked when he has to pay out, and laughs when the book keeps your stake.

He comments on your pick: the favourite is "Safe as houses", a long shot at 8/1 or more gets "A long shot. I like your nerve, nya.", and anything else is "Sensible choice." When you win: "Blimey! Take your winnings before I change my mind." When you lose: "The book thanks you, Mayor. Better luck next race, mrow."

## Races and the newspaper
Your race record (net winnings, races bet, races won, best win) is kept with the city in the save, together with the casino games. A single race that swings the treasury by $25,000, or by a quarter of what the city had (and at least $2,000), makes the next newspaper, such as "Lavender Lightning Wins At Vibe Downs And The City Collects $X" or "Mayor Loses $X At Kitty Meadows". If the city drops under $1,000 from it, the headline is "Mayor Loses The Treasury At The Races". Race losses also count toward the paper's "gambling losses" stories along with the casino.

## Keyboard controls at the Track
- 1 to 5: pick the horse in that stall (while betting).
- Enter or Space: start the race when betting; go to the next race when it is over.
- Escape: close the Track window (a running race still settles).
Everything can also be done with the mouse: click a horse on the card, click chips to build the stake, click Clear to reset it, click They're Off to run the race and Next race for a new card. The window can be dragged by its title bar so you can watch the city behind it.

## Common questions
**Q: How big is the Track and what does it need?**
A: A 3 by 3 lot on clear, level, dry ground. $10,000, $50 a month, power and water.

**Q: Does the Track cause crime?**
A: No. It gives a growth boost within 8 tiles and 24 jobs, with no crime.

**Q: How much does a winning bet pay?**
A: Stake times the odds plus the stake. $1,000 at 5/2 returns $3,500.

**Q: Can I bet on a horse to place or show?**
A: No. Only win bets. A horse that finishes second pays nothing.

**Q: What is the minimum and maximum bet?**
A: $100 minimum, $50,000 maximum, and never more than the city has.

**Q: Can I tell who will win from how they run?**
A: No. The result is drawn when they are off; the running is only a picture of it.

**Q: Is the favourite more likely to win?**
A: Yes. Prices come from each horse's hidden form, so shorter odds mean a better chance.

**Q: What happens if I close the window during a race?**
A: The race settles anyway and you are paid if your horse won.

**Q: How many horses run in each race?**
A: Five.
