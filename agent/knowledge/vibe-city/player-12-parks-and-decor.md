# Vibe City: Parks, Plazas, Statues and Trees
Audience: player
Keywords: park, parks, plaza, statue, tree, trees, decor, decoration, green space, amenity, growth boost, park picture, park window, kids in the park, fair, festival, clean air, pollution, upkeep, landscaping, Kit, Mochi, Pip, Rue
Summary: Parks, plazas, statues and trees are the cheap decor pieces in Vibe City. They give a small growth boost to lots within 4 tiles, trees also clean the air, and parks and plazas are where a fair can set up. Clicking a park with the pan tool opens a picture with one of the arcade kids talking to you.

## What the decor pieces are
In Vibe City the small decor pieces are the Park, the Plaza and the Statue, which live in the Decor drawer of the build menu, and the Tree, which lives in the Land drawer. All four are one tile each and go on clear grass. The Decor drawer also holds the bigger venues (Art Gallery, Casino, Club, Track and Arcade), which have their own manual pages.

The prices and running costs are:
- Park: $250 per tile, $4 a month upkeep.
- Plaza: $400, $6 a month upkeep.
- Statue: $1,000, $1 a month upkeep.
- Tree: $20, no monthly upkeep.

On hilly ground (the upper hill tiers) every building, decor included, costs 1.6 times the listed price. The monthly cost of parks, plazas and statues shows in City Stats on the "Decor upkeep" line. None of these pieces needs power or water, and none of them makes crime.

## The growth boost from decor
Every park, plaza, statue and tree in Vibe City is an amenity with a reach of 4 tiles. Any zoned lot inside that reach counts as "near an amenity". A lot near an amenity grows faster: its chance to level up is multiplied by an extra 0.15 (on top of the 0.3 a lot gets from being covered by a civic service such as police or a school), and its chance to shrink is cut by 0.1. The boost does not stack: one park or twenty in reach gives the same bonus, so spread your decor out to cover more lots rather than piling it in one place.

The bigger venues reach further. The Art Gallery and the Track reach 8 tiles, the Casino and the Club reach 6, and the Arcade starts at 6. A small park is the cheapest way to touch a pocket of houses the big venues miss.

## Trees clean the air
A tree in Vibe City does two jobs. Like the other decor it gives the 4 tile growth boost, and it also cleans the air. Each tree takes 6 points off the pollution field on its own tile, falling to nothing 2 tiles away, and it never pushes pollution below zero. One tree is a small help; a belt of trees between a factory row and the houses can take a heavy street down to a light one.

Trees are cheap at $20, and there are 16 different tree pictures, picked at random as you plant them. You do not need to clear trees before building: placing a building on a tree tile simply takes the tree down. The New City window also has a Trees setting (None, Sparse, Wooded, Forest) for how much woodland a new map starts with.

## Looks and variants
Parks, plazas and statues in Vibe City each come in four designs. Each time you place one, the game rolls one of the four designs at random, and the design is kept in your save so the building looks the same when you load the city again. You cannot pick the design yourself; if you want a different look, bulldoze ($5) and place it again. The design is only looks: every park design gives the same 4 tile boost at the same $4 a month, and the same is true for plazas and statues.

## Fairs on parks and plazas
Parks and plazas are the only places a fair can set up in Vibe City. When the city is well run (the mood score at 0.62 or better, out of 1; the "Content" face starts at 0.68) and has at least 300 people, there is a small chance each month (0.45%, scaled by the Random events slider in Settings) that a fair opens on a random park or plaza. A toast tells you where.

While the fair runs, lots within 5 tiles get extra demand, strongest at the centre and fading to nothing at the edge: up to +0.22 for commercial lots and +0.12 for homes, and nothing for industry. The newspaper runs cheerful headlines about it. A fair lasts a short while, and at least 30 months pass before the next one. If the park or plaza under it is bulldozed, the fair ends. Setting Random events to 0 turns fairs off along with the bad events.

## Fire and decor
Parks and trees can burn in Vibe City. A fire spreading from a burning neighbour catches a tree or park less readily than a row of houses (0.4 times the usual chance per try), so woods and parks slow a fire rather than carry it across the map. A burning tree or park can still light a wooden power pole beside it. Keep a fire station in reach of large wooded areas near your town. Every fire also runs out of steam on its own after 60 tiles have caught or 180 seconds have passed, and rain from a storm halves how fast it spreads.

## Clicking a park: the picture and the kids
In Vibe City you can visit a park. Choose the pan tool and click a park tile once. A window called "In the Park" opens with a picture from the park album and one of the arcade kids (Kit, Mochi, Pip or Rue) beside it, talking to you in a speech bubble. The kids call you "Mayor" and sometimes mention your city's name or its population.

How the window works:
- Click the picture, or press the Right arrow or Space, to draw another picture. It is always a different one from the last when there is more than one.
- While the window stays open, the kid says something new every 60 seconds.
- Escape or the close button shuts the window.
- The window can be dragged by its title bar.

Each picture belongs to someone. A picture of Kit chasing a sparrow brings Kit, and he talks about that picture first; after that he mixes in his everyday chat. Many pictures show other people of the city (dealers from the casino, the bookies from the Track, the ticket clerks from the station, the advisor, the archivist, the teacher, neighbours and their kids) and one of the arcade kids comments on them.

## Who talks in the park, and Rue
Only the kids you have met at the arcade can turn up in the park. Kit, Mochi and Pip are always available. Rue, Kit's big brother, only appears after you have beaten Kit all the way down at Neon Trails in the arcade (Kit's ladder at "Dethroned"). Until then, a picture that belongs to Rue is talked about by another kid instead, and for some pictures of Rue himself, Kit talks about his brother ("That's my brother Rue. He feeds the ducks every night."). Once Rue is unlocked, he speaks over his own pictures.

Each kid has a voice: Kit is cocky and competitive, Mochi is sweet and excitable, Pip is all about racing and being fastest, and Rue is calm and quiet. See the Characters page for more.

## Where the park pictures come from
The park album ships with the game. In the desktop app the game reads the whole parks folder as it starts, so every picture is there. Unlike the Art Gallery and the Club, the parks folder is not a player folder: you cannot add your own park pictures, because each picture is written for, with its kid and its lines. If the album is empty the window says "No pictures here yet." In the browser version, only the pictures listed with the game when it was put together appear; the desktop app always shows the whole album.

## Common questions
**Q: How much does a park cost in Vibe City?**
A: $250 per tile to build and $4 a month to keep. On high hill ground the build price is 1.6 times that.

**Q: How far does a park's growth boost reach?**
A: 4 tiles. Plazas, statues and trees reach 4 tiles too. The boost does not stack, so one piece in reach is enough.

**Q: Do trees cost upkeep?**
A: No. A tree costs $20 once and nothing per month.

**Q: Do trees reduce pollution?**
A: Yes. Each tree removes 6 points of pollution on its tile, fading to nothing 2 tiles away.

**Q: How do I see the kids in the park?**
A: Pick the pan tool and click a park tile once. The "In the Park" window opens with a picture and one of the kids talking.

**Q: How do I get a new picture in the park window?**
A: Click the picture, or press Right arrow or Space.

**Q: Why does Rue never talk in the park?**
A: Rue is locked until you beat Kit all the way down (to "Dethroned") at Neon Trails in the arcade.

**Q: Can I add my own pictures to the parks?**
A: No. The parks folder is not a player folder; its pictures ship with the game. You can add your own pictures to the Art Gallery instead (desktop app: Documents/Vibe City/gallery).

**Q: Where can a fair happen?**
A: Only on a park or a plaza, when the city is happy (mood 0.62 or more) and has at least 300 people.

**Q: Can I choose which park design I get?**
A: No. Parks, plazas and statues roll one of four designs at random when placed.
