# Vibe City: Money, Taxes, Budget and Milestones
Audience: player
Keywords: money, funds, treasury, budget, tax, tax rate, taxes, income, expenses, upkeep, maintenance, starting money, difficulty, net per month, deficit, debt, loan, milestones, rank, gift, City Stats, mayor's report, demand
Summary: In Vibe City you start with $10,000, $20,000 or $50,000 depending on difficulty, and earn money each month from a single tax rate on residents and jobs. Upkeep for roads, utilities, civic buildings and decor is paid monthly. Population and age milestones pay cash gifts, and the City Stats panel shows the full budget, tax slider and the advisor.

## Starting money and difficulty
When you make a new city in Vibe City, the New City window lets you choose how much money you start with:

- Hard: $10,000. "Every dollar counts."
- Normal: $20,000. The usual game.
- Easy: $50,000. Room to experiment.

Your money (the treasury) is shown in the top bar and turns red when it is below zero. The tax rate starts at 8%. The calendar starts in January of year 1.

Every build costs money up front, and the price shows on the drag plan before you let go. If you cannot pay, the plan turns red and the click does nothing, with the message "Insufficient funds". Bulldozing costs $5 a tile. There are no refunds for demolishing anything.

## How income works
Vibe City has one tax rate, set with the slider in the City Stats panel, from 0% to 25%. Each month the city collects:

- From residents: population x 0.6 x tax rate x 10.
- From jobs: (commercial jobs + industrial jobs) x 0.4 x tax rate x 10.

For example, a city of 1,000 people with 1,000 jobs at 8% tax collects (600 + 400) x 0.08 x 10 = $800 a month.

Population is 10 people per residential level. Jobs are 8 per commercial level and 10 per industrial level, plus fixed jobs from open businesses: casino or club 12 each, Track 24, Arcade 14, Seaport 20, Prison 30. So income grows with the city; there is no other regular income.

Other money comes from milestone gifts, a neighbouring town paying $2,500 when you answer its request for a link, and getting stolen money back when police catch a robber.

## Taxes and growth
The tax rate in Vibe City is a trade between income and growth. Higher taxes lower demand for every zone:

- From 10% commercial demand has nothing left pushing it up on its own.
- From 12.5% residential demand runs out.
- From 16.5% even industry no longer wants to build.

The note under the slider explains where you stand, for example "Every kind of lot still wants to be built at this rate" or "Nothing wants to be built at this rate. The city will start emptying out." At 0% nothing is collected at all.

Taxes also affect the city mood (18% of the score). Up to 7% people do not mind; from 7% to 20% the tax share falls steadily, and at 20% or more it is as bad as it gets ("Taxes are biting"). The Demand panel warns when taxes are at 12% or higher, and the newspaper runs "Taxpayers In Revolt" at 15% or more and "Tax Cuts Cheered" at 3% or less.

## Upkeep and expenses
Upkeep is charged every month. The City Stats budget groups it into four lines:

Road upkeep: street $1 a tile, avenue $2, highway $4, rail and level crossings $2 a tile, rail depot $25.

Civic upkeep: power plant $50, solar farm $5, wind farm $8, police $40, fire station $40, school $30, hospital $45, university $90, library $20, prison $60, art gallery $25, casino $30, club $30, track $50, arcade $60, seaport $40, plus any mod buildings.

Decor upkeep: park $4, plaza $6, statue $1, power line $1 a tile. Trees cost nothing to keep.

Water upkeep: pump $35, water tower $6, pipe $1 a tile.

The monthly net is total income minus total expenses, added to your funds once a month. Dragging the tax slider updates the budget figures live so you can see the effect before the month ends.

## Running out of money
Vibe City has no loans or bonds. If your net per month is negative, the treasury shrinks every month, and the upkeep keeps being charged even when the treasury is below zero, so funds can go negative. A robbery also takes $1,000 straight out of the till.

While you do not have enough money for a build, you cannot build it. To recover: raise taxes a little (watching demand), bulldoze expensive buildings you do not need, or let the city grow into its services. The newspaper notices: "City Runs In The Red" when the monthly net is negative, "Treasury Nearly Empty" when funds are under $1,000, and "Coffers Overflowing" when net is over $400 with more than 200 people.

## The City Stats panel
Open City Stats with its button on the top bar. It is laid out as the mayor's office:

- The advisor stands on the left with the city mood, a line from him, and the main reason for the mood.
- The left page, "The Mayor's Report", shows Budget (income from residents, income from commercial and industrial, total income, road, civic, decor and water upkeep, total expenses, net per month), Population (residents, commercial jobs, industrial jobs), Pollution and Crime.
- The right page, "Works and Services", shows Power, Water and Transport.
- Underneath are the tax slider with its note, the City history button (milestones) and the Neighbours button.
- The footer shows the city's rank and population, the treasury and net per month.

The figures update every month, and the panel stays live while open.

## Milestones and gifts
Milestones are one-time marks in Vibe City. Each pays a gift from the council, is written into the city's history and makes the newspaper for 3 editions. Population milestones also give the city a new rank:

- 500 people: "People Are Moving In", rank Village, $2,500.
- 2,000 people: "A Town At Last", rank Town, $6,000.
- 4,000 people: "It's A City", rank City, $12,000.
- 9,000 people: "The Capital", rank Capital, $25,000.
- 12,000 people: "A Metropolis", rank Metropolis, $40,000.

Anniversaries: 50 years ($5,000), 100 years ($12,000) and 250 years ($25,000).

When a milestone is reached the game pauses and a ceremony window shows it, presented by the archivist; Continue resumes at your previous speed. The City history button in City Stats lists every milestone passed with the date, and the total of gifts. A brand new city is a Village before its first mark.

In Vibe City milestones do not unlock buildings. Every tool in the build menu is available from the start, limited only by money and by building rules (a pump on the shore, a seaport on the coast, a depot beside track). Some needs appear with size instead: a coastal city past 2,500 people needs a seaport for industry to keep growing, and a city past 3,000 people needs a prison.

## The Demand meter and panel
The R, C and I bars in the top bar show current demand for residential, commercial and industrial building. Click them to open the Demand panel: a graph of the last 24 months, whether each line is rising, falling or steady, and a list of what is holding the city back, read out by the archivist. Typical entries are lots off the road, lots without power, taxes at 12% or more, more people than jobs, lots held small without water, homes breathing smog or on rough streets, and missing neighbour links.

## Common questions
**Q: How much money do I start with?**
A: $10,000 on Hard, $20,000 on Normal and $50,000 on Easy, chosen in the New City window.

**Q: How is tax income calculated?**
A: Each month: (population x 0.6 + jobs x 0.4) x tax rate x 10. At 8% tax, 1,000 people and 1,000 jobs bring in $800.

**Q: What is a good tax rate?**
A: Up to 7% does not hurt the mood. Commercial demand weakens from 10%, residential from 12.5%, industry from 16.5%. The default 8% is a reasonable start.

**Q: Can I take out a loan?**
A: No. Vibe City has no loans or bonds. Control your budget with the tax rate and upkeep.

**Q: What happens if my money goes negative?**
A: Upkeep keeps being charged and you cannot afford to build anything. Raise taxes, cut costly buildings, or grow the city to recover.

**Q: Which buildings cost the most to run?**
A: The university ($90 a month), the arcade and prison ($60), the power plant and track ($50) and the hospital ($45). Water pumps cost $35 each.

**Q: Do milestones unlock new buildings?**
A: No. All tools are available from the start. Milestones pay a cash gift, change your rank and go in the city history.

**Q: Where can I see my budget?**
A: In the City Stats panel, on the left page under Budget, with net per month in the footer.

**Q: Does bulldozing give money back?**
A: No. Bulldozing costs $5 a tile and refunds nothing.
