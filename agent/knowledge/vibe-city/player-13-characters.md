# Vibe City: The Characters
Audience: player
Keywords: characters, cast, people, NPC, Kit, Mochi, Pip, Rue, arcade kids, rival, dealer, dealers, cocoa, ginger, lilac, pink, snow, teal, bookie, bookies, advisor, archivist, teacher, ticket clerk, station clerk, unlock, who is who
Summary: Vibe City has a small cast of named characters who talk to you, the Mayor: four arcade kids, six casino dealers, two bookies at the Track, the advisor, the archivist, the teacher and five station ticket clerks. This page lists where each one appears, what they are like, and how the one unlockable character, Rue, is unlocked.

## The cast at a glance
In Vibe City every character addresses you as "Mayor". Most of the grown ups are cats in manner and sprinkle "nya", "mew" or "purr" into their lines. Here is where to find each of them:
- Kit, Mochi, Pip and Rue: the Arcade (click an Arcade with the pan tool), and the park pictures (click a park).
- The dealers (cocoa, ginger, lilac, pink, snow, teal): the Casino, at the blackjack and poker tables.
- The bookies (two of them): the Track, taking your race bets.
- The advisor: the City Stats window, next to the city's mood.
- The archivist: the Milestone and City History window, and the Demand window.
- The teacher: the classroom, opened by clicking a School or the University.
- The ticket clerks (five): the ticket office, opened by clicking a Rail Depot (station).
Many of the grown ups also turn up in the park pictures on their days off, with one of the kids talking about them.

## Kit, the rival kid
Kit owns the Arcade in Vibe City and is the first rival you meet there. He is a smug little brat who has lived in the arcade all summer: "My arcade, my rules. Pick a cabinet." His initials on the Maze Courier high score table are KIT, and his light colour in Neon Trails is hot pink.

Kit's Neon Trails ladder has four ranks: Rookie, Hotshot, Arcade King and Dethroned. Beat him in 3 matches at a rank and he moves up to the next, playing faster and smarter. When you beat him at Arcade King he becomes Dethroned and says he is fetching his big brother. In the parks Kit is cocky and competitive, forever chasing birds, squirrels and Pip, and calls the park "my turf".

## Mochi
Mochi is the sweetest of the arcade kids in Vibe City. She loves everything and everyone: "Hi hi! Wanna play with me?" Her initials are MOC and her light is yellow. Her ladder runs Newbie, Button Masher, Little Pro and Your Biggest Fan. She is the easiest Neon Trails opponent (her bike is the slowest and she wanders off course now and then). In the parks she makes flower crowns, rescues snails, names squirrels after you and reports how happy everyone looks.

## Pip
Pip is the kid having the best day ever. "Oh cool, you're here! Wanna race?" His initials are PIP and his light is orange. His ladder runs Tagalong, Speedrunner, Ace and Best Buds. He is a little faster than Mochi and slower than Kit at Neon Trails. In the parks Pip times everything, races toy cars and paper boats, and wants to race real cars at the Track when he grows up. He looks up to Kit ("I've been watching how Kit does it") and loves beating him even more.

## Rue, Kit's big brother (unlockable)
Rue is Kit's big brother and the arcade champion before him, who stopped coming in: "I used to own this place. Now I just sweep it." His initials are RUE, his light is purple, and his old score of 61,500 sits on top of the Maze Courier table where Kit cannot reach it. His ladder runs Retired, Warming Up, Old Legend and At Peace. He is the fastest and steadiest Neon Trails opponent and almost never slips.

How to unlock Rue: beat Kit all the way down at Neon Trails. That means winning 3 matches at each of Kit's first three ranks (9 match wins in all) so that Kit reaches Dethroned. The banner says "Someone just walked in behind him", and Rue's face appears in the roster under the card with a NEW tag. Until then his chip is a locked "???" saying "Beat Kit all the way down and his big brother shows up." Once unlocked, Rue also appears in the park pictures. In the parks he is calm and quiet, feeds the ducks, skates, and quietly roots for Kit.

## The casino dealers
In Vibe City the Casino's blackjack and poker tables are run by six dealers, known by the names cocoa, ginger, lilac, pink, snow and teal. Each time you walk into a casino a dealer is picked at random, never the one who dealt last time. The same dealer runs both card tables during that visit.

The dealers are cats about everything, calling you "hun" and "sweetie": "Place your bet, hun. Nya!", "House wins this one, sweetie. Nya ha!". Each dealer changes expression with the hand: a look for when the city is ahead, one for when the house wins, and some have a special face for a blackjack or a dealer bust. The faces differ by dealer: cocoa looks smug, pouts or goes wow; lilac smirks, laughs or winks; teal smiles, pouts or laughs.

In the park pictures the dealers appear on their own time: teal photographs the pond every morning, ginger takes the late tram home, lilac folds paper cranes, cocoa grows lavender by the cafe, pink buys flowers every week, and snow walks the river after her night shift.

## The bookies at the Track
The Track in Vibe City has two bookies. The game's pictures call them the brown bookie and the blond bookie. One of them is on the stand each time you open the Track, never the one who was there last time. The bookie writes your slip and has three moods: sly while he takes the bet, shocked when he has to pay out, and laughing when the book keeps your stake.

He talks like a racecourse bookmaker with a cat's purr: "Step up, step up! Who's your fancy today, Mayor?", "A long shot. I like your nerve, nya.", "Blimey! Take your winnings before I change my mind." In the park pictures the blond bookie drinks tea after the races and the brown bookie walks the canal after the last race.

## The advisor
The advisor is the Mayor's advisor in Vibe City. He lives in the City Stats window, beside the city's mood, and his face follows the mood: furious, unhappy, getting by, content or thriving. He says one line for each: from "They're furious, Mayor. Something has to change, and soon." up to "The city is thriving! Everyone loves you, Mayor. Mew!" Before anyone lives in town he says to zone some houses. In the park pictures he plays chess against himself after work.

## The archivist
The archivist keeps the city's ledger in Vibe City. She presents each milestone (a population rank or an anniversary) when it is reached, looking proud: "A new page for the ledger! Mew, Mayor, look at us!" She also sits over the City History list (opened from City Stats) with a calm face, and she presents the Demand window (opened by clicking the demand meter), where she reads out the biggest thing holding the city back, such as lots without power, high taxes, a missing seaport or a coming need for a prison. In the parks Mochi says the archivist remembers everything about the city and ends everything with "nya".

## The teacher
The teacher runs the classroom in Vibe City. Click a School or the University with the pan tool and she sets you two games, Chalkboard Maths and Word Scramble, a minute a round (easier at a school, harder at the university). She has six faces: smile, happy, proud, sigh, shock and stern, and her face follows how your round is going. She can be warm ("Purrfect!") or very stern ("Mayor. Did you even study? See me after class."). Your first round each month is graded in public: an A or B lifts the city's mood for a few months, a D or F costs it. If the school has no power or water she says the children went home. The kids call her "Miss".

## The station ticket clerks
Vibe City's stations are staffed by five ticket clerks. Click a Rail Depot with the pan tool and the ticket office opens with one of them at the counter. Each station keeps the same clerk every time, picked by where it stands, just as it keeps its name. The clerk tells you how many destinations there are, or that no trains call yet, or that the office is dark because the station has no power or water.

In the park pictures the five are called the brunette, ginger, lavender, pink and silver ticket ladies, and one picture shows all five having lunch together. They are railway people through and through: one reads timetables for fun, one waves at the last train every night.

## Common questions
**Q: How do I unlock Rue?**
A: Beat Kit at Neon Trails in the arcade until he is Dethroned: 3 match wins at each of his first three ranks, 9 in all.

**Q: Is Rue Kit's brother?**
A: Yes. Rue is Kit's big brother and the arcade champion before him.

**Q: Can I choose which casino dealer I get?**
A: No. A dealer is picked at random each time you enter a casino, never the same one twice in a row.

**Q: Where is the advisor?**
A: In the City Stats window, next to the city's mood. His face and line follow the mood.

**Q: Who presents milestones?**
A: The archivist. She also presents City History and the Demand window.

**Q: Does each station always have the same clerk?**
A: Yes. A station's clerk is picked by its position, so you find the same one every visit.

**Q: Which characters can appear in the park window?**
A: Only the arcade kids speak in the park: Kit, Mochi, Pip, and Rue once unlocked. The grown ups appear in the pictures.

**Q: How many dealers and bookies are there?**
A: Six dealers (cocoa, ginger, lilac, pink, snow, teal) and two bookies.

**Q: Does losing to the teacher matter?**
A: Only your first round each month counts. A D or F lowers the city's mood for a few months; an A or B raises it.
