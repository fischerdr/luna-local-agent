# Vibe City: The Casino (Blackjack, Poker and Slots)
Audience: player
Keywords: casino, gambling, blackjack, twenty one, poker, casino hold'em, hold em, slots, slot machine, jackpot, bet, ante, chips, payout, odds, house edge, dealer, dealers, city money, treasury, newspaper, nightlife, crime
Summary: The Casino is a $5,000 nightlife building in Vibe City. Click it with the pan tool to play blackjack, poker (Casino Hold'em) or the slot machine with the city's own money. Every bet comes out of the treasury and every win goes back into it, and big swings make the newspaper.

## The Casino building
In Vibe City the Casino is in the Decor drawer of the build menu (its tool is labelled Casino). It is one tile and costs $5,000 (1.6 times that on high hill ground), with $30 a month upkeep. It needs power (10 MW) and water (10 units) to open. An open casino:
- counts as nightlife: lots within 6 tiles grow faster, and homes in reach fill the city mood's "nightlife" share,
- gives 12 jobs,
- makes crime in the ground around it, spreading 5 tiles. A working police station in reach cuts that by 70%.
Casinos come in four looks, each with its own name: The Pink Box, The Purple Dome, The Diamond Terrace and The Heart. Every casino has all three games, one tab each.

## Going inside and the city's money
In Vibe City, choose the pan tool and click a Casino once. The window opens on whichever game tab you played last: Blackjack, Slots or Poker. A dealer is picked at random each visit (never the same one twice running) and runs both card tables.

The stakes are the city's money. A bet leaves the treasury the moment the cards are dealt or the lever is pulled, and whatever the hand pays goes straight back in. The footer shows the stake, the city's funds, and your running record for that game ("Up $X over N hands"). You cannot bet more than the city has. Escape or the close button leaves. Walking away in the middle of a blackjack hand stands on it and settles it; walking away mid poker hand folds it; a slot pull is already paid, so leaving mid spin loses nothing extra.

## Blackjack: rules
Vibe City's blackjack uses the common Las Vegas Strip rules:
- Six decks, reshuffled once three quarters of the shoe has been used.
- Blackjack (an ace and a ten card as your first two) pays 3 to 2.
- The dealer stands on every 17, soft 17 included.
- The dealer peeks for blackjack when showing an ace or a ten card. A dealer blackjack takes every bet except a player blackjack, which pushes (your bet back).
- You may double down on any first two cards, including after a split. Doubling doubles your bet and gives you exactly one more card.
- You may split any pair once (two cards of the same value). Split aces get one card each and no more, and an ace and a ten after a split counts as 21, not blackjack.
- There is no insurance and no surrender.
A win pays even money (your bet back plus the same again), a push returns your bet, a loss or bust loses it.

## Blackjack: betting and controls
Bets in Vibe City's blackjack go from $100 to $50,000, never more than the city has. Click chips worth $100, $500, $1K, $5K or $25K to build your bet, Clear to start over, then Deal. After a hand, the same bet is ready again if the city can still cover it.

Keyboard:
- Enter or Space: deal, and start the next round when a hand is done.
- H: hit. S: stand. D: double down. P: split.
- Escape: leave the casino.
Played well, blackjack gives back nearly every dollar you put in: it is the best game in the house.

## Poker (Casino Hold'em): rules
The Poker tab in Vibe City is Casino Hold'em: you play the dealer, nobody else.
1. Put down an ante and deal. You and the dealer get two cards each (hers face down), and three cards come out on the board (the flop).
2. Fold, losing the ante, or Call for twice the ante.
3. On a call the last two board cards come out and the dealer shows. Each side plays its best five cards from its two plus the five on the board.
4. The dealer must qualify with a pair of fours or better. If she does not, your ante is paid by the table below and your call comes back.
5. If she qualifies, the better hand wins: the ante is paid by the table and the call pays 1 to 1. If she wins, you lose both. A tie gives both back.

## Poker: ante payouts and controls
When your ante wins in Vibe City's poker, it pays by your hand:
- Royal flush: 100 to 1
- Straight flush: 20 to 1
- Four of a kind: 10 to 1
- Full house: 3 to 1
- Flush: 2 to 1
- Straight or less: 1 to 1
The call always pays 1 to 1.

Antes go from $100 to $10,000, using chips of $100, $500, $1K and $5K. Because a call costs twice the ante, the deal waits until the city could cover both: the ante can be at most a third of the city's funds. Keys: Enter or Space to deal and for the next hand, C to call, F to fold, Escape to leave. Played well, poker pays back about 98 cents on the dollar.

## The slot machine
The Slots tab in Vibe City is an old three reel machine with one payline (the middle line). Pick a bet of $100, $500, $1K or $5K and pull the lever, click Spin, or press Space or Enter. The bet leaves the treasury and the win comes back in the same moment. The pay table, as multiples of your bet (stake included):
- Three diamonds: 500x, the jackpot
- Three sevens: 80x
- Three bars: 30x
- Three bells: 15x
- Three plums: 10x
- Three cherries: 10x
- Three lemons: 8x
- Any mix of bars, sevens and diamonds: 5x
- Two cherries anywhere on the line: 3x
- One cherry anywhere: your bet back
A win rings, the jackpot rings longer, and a loss is silent. The machine hits often (something lands on more than a third of pulls) but pays back only about 92 cents on the dollar: the slow drain of the house.

## The dealers
The six Vibe City dealers are cocoa, ginger, lilac, pink, snow and teal. The dealer behind the felt talks through every hand in a cat's purr ("Hit or stand, hun?", "Blackjack! Lucky paws, nya!", "Dealer blackjack. Sorry, hun. Mew.") and changes expression: pleased when the city comes out ahead, smug when the house wins, and some have a special face for a blackjack or a dealer bust. When the city is short of the minimum she says "Come back when the city's flush. Mew." See the Characters page for more about them.

## The newspaper and your gambling record
Vibe City keeps your gambling record with the city in the save. A single hand, pull or race that swings the treasury by $25,000, or by a quarter of what the city had (and at least $2,000), makes the next newspaper ("Mayor Wins $X At Blackjack", "Mayor Loses $X Of City Money At Poker"). A slots jackpot always makes the paper. The paper also notices patterns: losses of $10,000 or more over the last three editions ("Mayor's Gambling Losses Mount"), wins of $10,000 or more ("The Casino Has Paid The City"), and a city under $1,000 that has lost money gambling ("City Broke, Mayor's Casino Habit Blamed").

## Common questions
**Q: Does the casino use my city's money?**
A: Yes. Every bet comes out of the treasury and every win goes back into it.

**Q: What is the best game to play?**
A: Blackjack played well returns nearly all of your money. Poker returns about 98%, the slots about 92%.

**Q: What are the blackjack bet limits?**
A: $100 minimum, $50,000 maximum, and never more than the city has.

**Q: What does blackjack pay?**
A: A natural blackjack pays 3 to 2. Other wins pay 1 to 1.

**Q: What does the slot jackpot pay?**
A: Three diamonds pays 500 times the bet.

**Q: When does the poker dealer qualify?**
A: With a pair of fours or better. If she does not, your ante is paid and your call comes back.

**Q: What are the keyboard shortcuts in blackjack?**
A: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, Enter or Space to deal or go again, Escape to leave.

**Q: Does the casino cause crime?**
A: Yes. An open casino puts crime into the ground within 5 tiles. A police station in reach cuts it by 70%.

**Q: What happens if I close the window mid hand?**
A: Blackjack stands and settles the hand; poker folds; a slot pull was already paid.
