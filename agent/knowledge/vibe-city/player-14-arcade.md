# Vibe City: The Arcade
Audience: player
Keywords: arcade, arcade building, Neon Trails, Maze Courier, light cycle, tron, pac man, maze chase, high score, rival, Kit, Mochi, Pip, Rue, ranks, ladder, unlock, crowd draw, amenity reach, controls, keys, wardens, permits, pizza
Summary: The Arcade is a $9,000 one tile venue in Vibe City that boosts growth around it with no crime. Click it with the pan tool to play two cabinets, Neon Trails and Maze Courier, against the arcade kids. Beating the kids at Neon Trails moves them up their ladders, unlocks Rue, and makes every arcade in town reach further.

## The Arcade building
The Arcade is in the Decor drawer of the Vibe City build menu, after the Track. It is one tile and costs $9,000 (1.6 times that on high hill ground), with $60 a month upkeep. It needs power (12 MW) and water (8 units) to be open. An open arcade:
- gives 14 jobs,
- boosts growth for zoned lots within 6 tiles, plus the crowd bonus described below, up to 11 tiles,
- makes no crime, unlike the Casino and the Club.
A shut arcade (no power or water) gives no jobs and no boost. The tile inspector shows its amenity radius, its jobs, and "Inside: Click to play". Playing the cabinets costs nothing: no city money is at stake in the arcade.

## Going inside
In Vibe City, choose the pan tool and click an Arcade tile once. The arcade window opens. On the left is the current rival in a lit card with a speech bubble, and a row of faces under it (the roster). On the right are two cabinets: Neon Trails and Maze Courier. The footer shows your Trails record (matches won and lost against everyone), the Crowd draw (the arcade's current reach in tiles), and the number of games played.

Click a face in the roster to pick who you play. Hover a face to see that kid's rank and your record against them. You cannot change rival while a game is running. Click a cabinet to start it. Escape leaves a running game and goes back to the cabinets; Escape again closes the arcade.

## The kids you play
The Vibe City arcade has four regulars, each with a Neon Trails ladder of four ranks:
- Mochi: Newbie, Button Masher, Little Pro, Your Biggest Fan. The easiest.
- Pip: Tagalong, Speedrunner, Ace, Best Buds.
- Kit, who owns the arcade: Rookie, Hotshot, Arcade King, Dethroned.
- Rue, Kit's big brother: Retired, Warming Up, Old Legend, At Peace. The hardest, and locked at first.
Mostly what makes a kid harder is speed: Mochi's bike is the slowest and Rue's the fastest. Mochi and Pip also wander off a safe way now and then, while Kit from Arcade King and Rue look a second step ahead and almost never slip. Each kid talks the whole time: hellos, taunts when they win a round, sulks when you do.

## Neon Trails: rules and controls
Neon Trails in Vibe City is a light cycle duel. You and the rival each ride a bike that leaves a solid wall of light behind it. Your bike is cyan; the rival's is their own colour. Crash into any wall (the arena edge, your own trail or theirs) and you lose the round. If both crash on the same step, the round is a draw and nobody scores. First to 3 rounds wins the match.

Controls:
- Arrow keys or W, A, S, D to turn. You cannot reverse straight back on yourself. Up to three turns can be queued.
- Space pauses and resumes.
- Enter starts a rematch when a match is over.
- Escape goes back to the cabinets.
Each round starts with a short countdown from 3. Your bike starts on the left heading right, the rival on the right heading left.

## How to beat the kids at Neon Trails
In Vibe City a rival always protects their own room and never steers into a wall on purpose, so the way to win is to take space from them:
- Cut across in front of the rival early to split the arena, leaving yourself the bigger half. Once you are walled apart, whoever has more room wins by outlasting.
- When sealed in a pocket, fill it tightly by hugging the walls, so you use every cell.
- Avoid moving into a cell right next to the rival's head: a head on crash is at best a draw.
- Start against Mochi or Pip. Their bikes are slower, giving you more time to react.
- Kit as a Rookie is passive. From Hotshot on he fights for space, and at Arcade King he looks a step ahead.
- Rue is faster still. Expect to practise.

## Ranks, unlocks and the crowd reward
Each match you win at Neon Trails counts toward that kid's next rank. Win 3 matches at a rank and they move up to the next, playing harder. A kid who reaches their fourth rank (Your Biggest Fan, Best Buds, Dethroned or At Peace) has been beaten all the way and stays there. Losses do not move a kid down.

Unlocking Rue: when Kit reaches Dethroned (9 match wins against Kit in all), Rue walks in. His face appears in the roster with a NEW tag. Rue also starts appearing in the park pictures.

The reward for beating the kids is a bigger crowd. Every rank any kid has moved up adds 1 tile to the reach of every arcade in your city, up to 5 extra tiles. So the arcade's growth reach goes from 6 tiles to a maximum of 11. The footer's "Crowd draw" shows the current figure. Your ladders and records are part of the city and go in your save.

## Maze Courier: rules
Maze Courier in Vibe City is a maze chase. You ride a courier scooter through a city at night, collecting every coin on every street, while four traffic wardens patrol. Touch a warden and you lose a life. Clear every coin to finish the level, and the next level has a new town layout.

The details:
- You start with 3 lives, and get an extra life every 10,000 points.
- Coins are worth 10 points.
- Four parking permits (marked P) sit in the corners. A permit is worth 50 points and puts every warden off duty: they turn blue and run. Off duty lasts 7 seconds on level 1, 0.9 seconds less each level, never under 1.5 seconds, and they blink in the last 2 seconds.
- Catching off duty wardens in a row scores 200, 400, 800, then 1,600. A caught warden goes back to the pen and comes out again shortly.
- A pizza appears at your start point after you collect 70 coins and again after 170, for 9.5 seconds. It is worth 100 on level 1, rising to 300, 500, 700, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 and 5,000 on later levels.
- Clearing a level is worth 500 times the level number.
- Tunnels at both ends of the middle row wrap round to the other side. Wardens slow down in them; there are no coins in the tunnels.

## Maze Courier: wardens, controls and tips
The four wardens in Vibe City's Maze Courier each have a habit. Red heads straight for you. Amber aims 4 cells ahead of where you are going. Blue tries to pinch you from the far side of the red one. Violet comes close, then loses its nerve and backs off. They alternate between scattering to their corners and chasing (7 seconds scatter, 20 chase, repeating, until they chase for good), and every warden turns round when the phase changes. At the start one warden is already out and one leaves the pen at once; the other two come out after about 4 and 8 seconds, or sooner once you have collected 30 and 60 coins.

Controls: arrow keys or W, A, S, D to steer. You can turn back at any time, even mid street. Space pauses.

Tips: save a permit until wardens are close, then turn and eat them for the chain bonus. Use the tunnels to shake a chaser. Grab the pizza when it appears at the start.

## The high score table
Maze Courier keeps a table of the 6 best scores in your city. A new city starts with the kids' scores: RUE 61,500, KIT 48,200, KIT 31,500, PIP 22,000, KIT 12,800 and MOC 6,400. If your score makes the table, type up to three initials (letter keys, Backspace to fix) and press Enter. Press Enter on the table to play again. Whoever is at the cabinet judges your score against their own best. Maze Courier scores do not change the kids' ranks; only Neon Trails does.

## Common questions
**Q: How much does the Arcade cost?**
A: $9,000 to build and $60 a month. It needs power and water.

**Q: Does the arcade cause crime?**
A: No. Unlike the Casino and the Club, the Arcade makes no crime.

**Q: Does playing the arcade cost city money?**
A: No. The arcade games are free; nothing is bet.

**Q: What are the controls?**
A: Arrow keys or WASD to steer, Space to pause, Enter for a rematch or to play again, Escape to leave the game and then the arcade.

**Q: How do I unlock Rue?**
A: Beat Kit at Neon Trails until he is Dethroned: 3 match wins at each of his first three ranks.

**Q: What do I get for beating the kids?**
A: Each rank a kid moves up adds 1 tile of reach to every arcade, up to +5, so arcades can reach 11 tiles.

**Q: Which kid is easiest?**
A: Mochi. Her bike is the slowest and she wanders.

**Q: Does Maze Courier affect the ladders?**
A: No. Only Neon Trails matches move the kids up their ladders.

**Q: How many lives do I get in Maze Courier?**
A: Three, plus one every 10,000 points.
