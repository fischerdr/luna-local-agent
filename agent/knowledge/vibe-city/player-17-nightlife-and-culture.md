# Vibe City: Nightlife and Culture (Club, Gallery, Library, Radio and Sound)
Audience: player
Keywords: club, nightclub, dancers, dance, lounge, nightlife, crime, art gallery, gallery, pictures, library, culture, radio, music, music player, tape, soundtrack, volume, sound effects, sfx, interface sounds, settings, audio, mute
Summary: This page covers Vibe City's culture and nightlife buildings, the Club with its dancers, the Art Gallery and the Library, plus the Radio music player and the sound settings. The Club is nightlife that brings crime; the Gallery and Library boost growth without it. The Radio plays the soundtrack, and Settings has separate Music, Sound and Interface volumes.

## Nightlife in Vibe City
In Vibe City two buildings count as nightlife: the Casino and the Club. Nightlife does three things. Lots within 6 tiles of an open nightlife building grow faster, like lots near a park. Homes within 6 tiles count toward the city mood's "nightlife" share ("There is nothing to do at night" is the complaint when it is missing), worth 5% of the mood score. And nightlife makes crime: each open Casino or Club puts crime into the ground, spreading 5 tiles and fading with distance. A working police station in reach removes 70% of it. Crime cuts how much people want to live there and feeds the "safety" share of the mood, which is worth 7%. A shut club (no power or water) is neither fun nor trouble. The trade is yours: put a police station next door.

## The Club and its dancers
The Club is in the Decor drawer of the Vibe City build menu. It is one tile, costs $5,000 (1.6 times that on high hill ground) and $30 a month upkeep, and needs power (10 MW) and water (10 units). Open, it gives nightlife within 6 tiles, 12 jobs, and some crime. Each Club gets a lounge name by where it stands: Kitt Kat Lounge, Whisker Wonders, Sweet Illusions or Secret Lounge. Each lounge has its own room in its own colours: hot pink and chrome, purple and gold, teal and violet, or red and black.

To go in, choose the pan tool and click the Club once. The lights go down on the lounge's stage and one dancer performs at a time, a looping animation with spotlights, haze and drifting dust over the room. Use the arrow buttons, or the Left and Right arrow keys, to change dancer. The caption is the dancer file's name, and a counter shows which one of how many. Escape closes the window. A file that will not load is skipped.

## Adding your own dancers
The Vibe City Club shows whatever is in its clubs folder: animated GIF, WebP, PNG or APNG files. In the desktop app you can add your own: put them in Documents/Vibe City/clubs and reopen the game, and they show alongside the ones the game ships. If there are none at all, the stage says "The stage is empty tonight." The caption is the file name with the extension removed and dashes and underscores turned into spaces, so name your files the way you want them captioned. Reload the game (Ctrl+R) or restart it after adding files. In the browser version you cannot add dancers; only the ones listed with the game appear.

## The Art Gallery
The Art Gallery is a culture building in the Vibe City Decor drawer. It is one tile, costs $4,000 and $25 a month upkeep, and needs no power or water. It boosts growth for zoned lots within 8 tiles, twice a park's reach, and makes no crime. Galleries come in four designs, rolled at random when you build one.

To visit, choose the pan tool and click the Gallery once. A large window opens on the pictures in the gallery folder, one at a time, in a frame. Use the arrow buttons or the Left and Right arrow keys to turn the page; Escape closes it. The caption is the file name without its extension, with underscores and dashes as spaces, so "Harbour_at_dusk.png" hangs as "Harbour at dusk". The tile inspector shows "Exhibition: Click to visit".

## Adding your own pictures to the Gallery
In the Vibe City desktop app the Gallery is a player folder: put your own pictures (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or AVIF) in Documents/Vibe City/gallery and reopen the game, and they hang alongside the game's own. If the gallery is empty it says "Nothing is hanging yet." Screenshots of your city make good gallery pictures. Reload (Ctrl+R) or restart after adding files. In the browser version the gallery shows only the pictures listed with the game, so adding your own is a desktop app feature. The park pictures are different: they ship with the game and you cannot add to them.

## The Library
The Library is the small end of Vibe City's education ladder, found among the civic buildings. It is one tile, costs $900 and $20 a month upkeep, and needs power (6 MW) and water (8 units). It does a school's job over a smaller area: service coverage within 7 tiles, which helps lots grow (a school reaches 10 tiles, a university 16). It is cheap enough to drop into a neighbourhood the school does not quite reach. Libraries come in four designs. Unlike the gallery and the schools, the library has no window to click into.

## The Radio
The Radio is Vibe City's music player. Click the Radio button in the top bar to open it: a car stereo style head unit with a cassette deck. The controls:
- Play / Pause in the middle, Previous and Next track either side.
- VOL: the volume knob. It is the same level as the Music slider in Settings, so moving one moves the other.
- TAPE and RADIO buttons: they latch, but there is only one music source (the game's music folder), so they change nothing else.
The display scrolls the track name in capitals, the cassette label shows "TRACK n / total", and the spools turn with the real position in the song, stopping when the music pauses. With no music it reads "NO TAPE". Pressing play with the volume at zero turns it up to 60%.

## How the music plays
Vibe City's music plays the whole music folder in a shuffled order, and reshuffles each time it comes round, so it plays forever until the Music volume is at 0. Browsers do not allow sound before you interact with the page, so the music starts on your first click or key press. Setting Music to 0 pauses the song and keeps its place; raising it again carries on. A file that fails to load is skipped. In the desktop app the game reads the music folder itself as it starts.

## Sound settings
Settings in Vibe City has an Audio group with three sliders:
- Music (default 60%): the radio.
- Sound (default 80%): building, traffic, sirens and the rest of the city, including disasters, the casino, the races and the arcade's beeps.
- Interface (default 70%): menus and buttons, clicks, page turns and the newspaper.
Each slider plays a short sound at its new level when you let go, so you hear what you set. The sliders use a curve so the change is even across their travel. A slider at 0 is silent for that group. These settings are kept in your browser or app, not in a city save, so they stay the same when you load another city.

## The sound effects you will hear
Vibe City's effects are grouped:
- Building: placing and bulldozing.
- Events: fire sirens and crackle, thunder, the tornado, the flood, the earthquake, the meltdown alarm and blast, the UFO hum and death ray, police radio.
- Casino: cards dealt, the shuffle, the slot reels spinning, a ring for a win and a longer ring for the jackpot.
- Races: the bugle call to the post, the starting bell, galloping hooves, the crowd and the cheer at the line.
- School: the bell and a chime for a right answer.
- Interface: button clicks, page turns, tile select, slider previews and the newspaper opening.
Repeated sounds, like a road being dragged, are rate limited and slightly varied in pitch so they do not drone.

## Common questions
**Q: Does the Club cause crime?**
A: Yes. An open Club or Casino puts crime into the ground within 5 tiles. A working police station in reach removes 70% of it.

**Q: How do I see the dancers?**
A: Choose the pan tool and click a Club once. Change dancer with the arrows or the Left and Right keys.

**Q: How do I add my own pictures to the gallery?**
A: In the desktop app, put them in Documents/Vibe City/gallery and reopen the game.

**Q: Can I add my own dancers?**
A: Yes, in the desktop app: animated GIF, WebP, PNG or APNG files in Documents/Vibe City/clubs.

**Q: Does the Art Gallery need power?**
A: No. It needs neither power nor water and reaches 8 tiles.

**Q: What do the TAPE and RADIO buttons do?**
A: Nothing beyond lighting up. There is only one music source.

**Q: Why is there no music when the game opens?**
A: Browsers block sound until you interact. Music starts on your first click or key press, as long as Music is above 0.

**Q: How do I turn off the button clicks but keep the city sounds?**
A: Set Interface to 0 in Settings and leave Sound up.

**Q: Are my volume settings saved in my city file?**
A: No. They are kept by the browser or app and apply to every city.
