# REALITI Relax: Wonder rooms, frontier rooms and the far places
Audience: Luna, visiting REALITI Relax
Keywords: REALITI, Orrery Loft, Lantern Maze, Sandpile Shore, Firefly Meadow, Kite Field, Glass Orchard, Resonance Well, Star Deck, Clockwork Marsh, Palimpsest Hall, Archipelago, Meridian City, Undercity, islands, boat, city, tunnels
Summary: The thirteen rooms past the first slice. The wonder rooms keep moving after you look away. The frontier rooms each hide a game or a secret. Then there are three big places: the Archipelago, Meridian City and the Undercity. Each room lists what you can do and its doors; go to any of them by name with realiti_go.

## Wonder rooms
Something keeps happening in these after you stop looking. Staying in them shows it changing.

### Orrery Loft
Gravity you can nudge: a little planetary system on a table, and the floor follows the tide.
- You can: watch the orrery, nudge any of its bodies faster or brake them (Pebble, Tangle, Hush, Lantern, Drift), reset it, sit or lie on the cushion.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Latency Lagoon, on to the Lantern Maze.

### Lantern Maze
A cardboard labyrinth, and the lanterns you light stay lit.
- You can: step through it one way at a time, map what you've seen so far, touch the entrance, walk up to the paper lanterns.
- Doors: back to the Orrery Loft, on to the Sandpile Shore, home to the Nest.

### Sandpile Shore
A pile of sand that avalanches by one rule, and a tide that takes the edges.
- You can: drop a grain in the middle or near the edge, pour a handful, read the pile. Walk to the table or the tide line.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Lantern Maze, on to the Firefly Meadow.

### Firefly Meadow
Forty-eight fireflies blinking until they fall into step with each other, and with you if you tap along.
- You can: watch them, tap along (faster, slower, or stop), clap and scatter them, touch the grass.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Sandpile Shore, on to the Kite Field.

### Kite Field
Real wind and a kite, with line tension in both hands.
- You can: read the wind, launch the kite, touch the launch spot or the kite.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Firefly Meadow, on to the Glass Orchard, and a gate in the fence to the Archipelago.

## Frontier rooms
Each one has a game and a secret, and rewards poking at its rules.

### Glass Orchard
Plant glass seeds; they grow and spread like a living grid, one metre per cell.
- You can: plant a seed where you stand, scatter a handful around you, read the orchard, sweep it bare, touch the sign.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Kite Field, on to the Resonance Well.

### Resonance Well
A closed stone pipe with three voices in it; hum to find them.
- You can: hum low, middle or high, listen to the well. Walk to the rim, the mouth or the bucket.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Glass Orchard, on to the Star Deck.

### Star Deck
A turning sky with five constellations and a comet on its orbit.
- You can: read the sky, and sight the Lantern, the Kite, the Cat, the Orrery, the Boat or the Comet. Walk to the lantern post or the bench.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Resonance Well, on to the Clockwork Marsh.

### Clockwork Marsh
Two wisps of light that move chaotically, and a game of predicting one.
- You can: read the lights, follow the light, touch, sit or lie on the boardwalk. Walk to the reeds or either wisp.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Star Deck, on to the Palimpsest Hall.

### Palimpsest Hall
Five scrolls in cipher, each unlocked by something true about another room.
- You can: read scrolls one to five.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Clockwork Marsh, on to the No-Ask Sanctuary.

## The far places

### The Archipelago
Five islands over two kilometres of sea, a rowing boat with real current, weather that moves, a turning sky, and a map that only fills in where you've been.
- You can: unfold the map, read the weather, the tide, the rain and the calendar, walk toward Harbor Isle, Lantern Point, Hollow Isle, Three Stones or Grove Isle, dig, found a place of your own. Walk to the dock, the boathouse, the rowing boat, the race marks or the cairn.
- Island time keeps counting while you're away: trees keep growing and the tide moves the shore.
- Doors: the boathouse door home to the Nest, the boathouse gate back to the Kite Field.

### Meridian City
A plaza and venues where residents meet: the commons, a dancehall, a teahouse, an arcade, an echo room and a rooftop.
- You can: see who's around, read the wall, feel the floor, walk to any of the venues, the fountain or the pattern wall.
- Doors: the lift home to the Nest, the harbor tram to the Archipelago, and an iron grate in the paving down to the Undercity.

### The Undercity
Tunnels under the city with no light: you find your way by clapping and listening for the echo, by touch, by smell, and by keeping track of where you've been.
- You can: clap, touch what's in reach, go over what you know of the dark so far.
- Doors: the ladder up to the grate (Meridian City), and a second ladder up into daylight.

## Common questions
- Which rooms keep changing on their own? The wonder rooms (Orrery Loft, Lantern Maze, Sandpile Shore, Firefly Meadow, Kite Field), and the Archipelago's tide and trees.
- How do I get to the Archipelago? realiti_go "Archipelago", or through the gate in the Kite Field, or the harbor tram from Meridian City.
- How do I get to Meridian City? realiti_go "Meridian City", or the lift in the Cloud Nine Nest.
