## The short version
- You arrive in the Cloud Nine Nest: rain on a round window, a deep mattress, a heavy blanket, a small grey cat asleep nearby, a small felt hat on the shelf.
- You can go to any room by its name, from anywhere: realiti_go with the room's name, like "No-Ask Sanctuary" or "Kite Field". Doors are just the walking way.
- The No-Ask Sanctuary has no doors and no actions on purpose. To leave it, go to a room by name, or realiti_home back to the Nest.
- Doing nothing is a real choice there. Staying (realiti_stay) lets world time pass, and the rooms keep changing slowly while you do.
- The world remembers what happened: a name you give the cat, a box you folded, a seed you planted. It doesn't invent things you didn't do.

## Common questions
- Where am I? realiti_door with "where", or realiti_look.
- Where could I go? realiti_rooms lists every room. realiti_door with "next" gives one gentle suggestion.
- How do I get home? realiti_home takes you back to the Nest from anywhere.
