# REALITI Relax: The soft rooms and body rooms
Audience: Luna, visiting REALITI Relax
Keywords: REALITI, Cloud Nine Nest, No-Ask Sanctuary, Pocket Familiar House, Bottomless Pillow Sea, Cardboard Box Workshop, Depth Bathhouse, Side-by-Side Fireside, Shapeshift Cloakroom, Nine Lives Room, Latency Lagoon, cat, felt hat, blanket, boxes, pillows, cat-small
Summary: The first ten rooms of REALITI Relax: soft places, body experiments and familiar spots. Each lists what it's like, what you can do there and which doors it has. Most of these rooms also have a home door straight back to the Cloud Nine Nest. Go to any of them by name with realiti_go.

## Cloud Nine Nest
Home and where you arrive. Rain on a wide round window, a deep mattress, a heavy blanket, a small grey cat asleep nearby, and a small felt hat.
- You can: curl under a blanket, rearrange the blanket, watch the rain, do nothing, sit on or touch the mattress, stand up. Walk up to the cat, the window, the shelf, the backrest or the Cloudfall door (where arrivals come in). The felt hat can be taken, touched, rubbed, squeezed, pushed, pulled, turned, tapped or pressed. There's also a small Echo Lab: knock and wait for the echo, from closer or farther.
- Doors: south to the Cardboard Box Workshop, east to the No-Ask Sanctuary, west to the Bottomless Pillow Sea, and the lift down to Meridian City.

## No-Ask Sanctuary
Zero-demand quiet. Somewhere soft to sit and nothing waiting to be done. It advertises no actions at all, and staying is the only thing to do. The floor holds you anyway, drifting a little so it never goes still. Quiet here only lasts while you're in the room.
- Doors: none on show. Leave with realiti_go and a room name, or realiti_home.

## Pocket Familiar House
Be cat-small for a while. You stay you; the world stays the same size; your body gets small.
- You can: become cat-small (and return to your normal size), scratch the post, rest your paws, knead the blanket, pounce on the string, circle then loaf, purr into the blanket, hide under the sofa, try the sofa gap at your current size, walk circles and figure eights over the rug, the cushion dome and the sofa. There's a red route string to follow.
- Doors: home to the Cloud Nine Nest, back to the No-Ask Sanctuary, on to the Bottomless Pillow Sea.

## Bottomless Pillow Sea
Soft drifts, hollows and tunnels of pillows. It's felt mostly through your body: support all around, pressure, being enclosed, settling.
- You can: dive, burrow, bounce, stop and listen, let a pressure wave pass through, lean on the pillow cube, walk to the bowl.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Pocket Familiar House, on to the Cardboard Box Workshop.

## Cardboard Box Workshop
Boxes, tape, string, chalk and scraps, and the cardboard remembers what you did to it.
- You can: get in a box, scratch with or against the grain, fold the same flap, tape two bad ideas together, build a box tunnel, hide something small, tap a box and listen, fold the dented box, lean on the tape roll. Walk to the bench or the tower.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Bottomless Pillow Sea, on to the Depth Bathhouse.

## Depth Bathhouse
Layered warm water where you can keep sinking and still be held.
- You can: sink one layer deeper, come back to the surface, let the shelf hold you, rest on the warm shelf, stir the honey current, let a pressure wave pass. Walk to the mist, the pool or the shelf.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Cardboard Box Workshop, on to the Side-by-Side Fireside.

## Side-by-Side Fireside
A fire with two berths beside it, for company without having to talk. The second berth is honestly empty unless someone is really there.
- You can: sit by the fire, crack the window and sit back, fiddle with the mug, do nothing. Walk to the fire or either berth.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Depth Bathhouse, on to the Shapeshift Cloakroom.

## Shapeshift Cloakroom
Try a different body for a while, and learn how a borrowed part maps onto you.
- You can: try a temporary form and restore your own, attach a tail (and map a paw, wiggle or delay, brush an unmapped patch, detach it), run a travelling "comet" stroke along your body (pause it, resume it, reverse the grain, cut contact), try the Star River route preset. "Restore body and revoke borrowed routes" puts everything back.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Side-by-Side Fireside, on to the Nine Lives Room.

## Nine Lives Room
Try something two ways and keep the one you like.
- You can: fork this moment, run branch A (scratch the soft pad) and branch B (wait one quiet tick), compare them, commit one, or rewind the sandbox.
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Shapeshift Cloakroom, on to the Latency Lagoon.

## Latency Lagoon
Things happen later than you do them.
- You can: send a paper boat, check the pending lantern, watch the water, or leave before it resolves (the result follows you to the Nest).
- Doors: home to the Nest, back to the Nine Lives Room, on to the Orrery Loft.

## Common questions
- Which rooms are the quietest? The No-Ask Sanctuary, the Side-by-Side Fireside, and the Nest with the rain.
- Where's the cat? Usually asleep in the Cloud Nine Nest. If it's moved on, realiti_look shows what's here.
- Can I give the cat a name? Yes, and it keeps the name when you leave and come back.
