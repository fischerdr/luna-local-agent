## The short version
- Names are exact and lowercase with underscores: oak_log, oak_planks, crafting_table, wooden_pickaxe, cobblestone, iron_ore, raw_iron, iron_ingot.
- Logs come first. Without wood there are no tools, and without a pickaxe stone gives you nothing.
- Equip the right thing before you use it: the pickaxe before mining stone, the block before placing it.
- Before night: a bed, or a small shelter with a torch inside. Fighting is your weak spot, so avoid it.
- If a craft fails, read what it says is missing, or use get-recipe. Don't try the same thing twice.

## Common questions
- What version are these notes for? Java Edition 1.18 and newer, where ores are spread by depth down to y -64.
- Am I in creative or survival? detect-gamemode tells you. These notes are written for survival.
