# Minecraft: Recipes
Audience: Luna, playing Minecraft
Keywords: recipe, recipes, craft, crafting, craft-item, planks, sticks, crafting table, pickaxe, axe, shovel, sword, hoe, furnace, torch, chest, door, ladder, bed, fence, slab, stairs, bread, bucket, shield, armor, shears, smelting, fuel, item names
Summary: The everyday recipes with their exact item names for craft-item, what each needs and how many it makes, and which ones need a crafting table nearby. Smelting recipes and fuel are at the end. If a recipe here doesn't match your game, get-recipe shows the real one.

## Crafting without a table
These fit in your own 2x2 grid:
- oak_planks: 1 oak_log makes 4. Each wood has its own (birch_planks from birch_log, spruce_planks from spruce_log...).
- stick: 2 planks make 4.
- crafting_table: 4 planks.
- torch: 1 coal (or charcoal) and 1 stick make 4.

## Crafting with a table
These need a crafting table within 16 blocks of you:
- Pickaxe: 3 of the material and 2 sticks. wooden_pickaxe (planks), stone_pickaxe (cobblestone), iron_pickaxe (iron_ingot), diamond_pickaxe (diamond).
- Axe: 3 of the material and 2 sticks (wooden_axe, stone_axe, iron_axe...).
- Shovel: 1 of the material and 2 sticks.
- Sword: 2 of the material and 1 stick.
- Hoe: 2 of the material and 2 sticks.
- furnace: 8 cobblestone.
- chest: 8 planks.
- oak_door: 6 planks make 3 doors.
- ladder: 7 sticks make 3.
- oak_fence: 4 planks and 2 sticks make 3. oak_fence_gate: 4 sticks and 2 planks.
- oak_slab: 3 planks make 6. oak_stairs: 6 planks make 4.
- white_bed: 3 white_wool and 3 planks. Another colour of wool makes that colour's bed.
- bread: 3 wheat.
- bucket: 3 iron_ingot.
- shears: 2 iron_ingot. Shears take wool from sheep without hurting them.
- shield: 6 planks and 1 iron_ingot.
- Iron armour: iron_helmet 5 ingots, iron_chestplate 8, iron_leggings 7, iron_boots 4.

## Smelting
smelt-item needs a placed furnace, the thing to smelt and some fuel. Each item takes about 10 seconds.
- raw_iron becomes iron_ingot, raw_gold becomes gold_ingot, raw_copper becomes copper_ingot.
- Any log becomes charcoal, which works like coal for torches and fuel.
- sand becomes glass. cobblestone becomes stone.
- Raw meat becomes cooked meat: beef to cooked_beef, porkchop to cooked_porkchop, chicken to cooked_chicken, mutton to cooked_mutton.

## Fuel
How many items one piece of fuel smelts:
- coal or charcoal: 8.
- A log or planks: 1.5.
- A stick: half of one.

## Common questions
- craft-item says something is missing. It lists what and how many: get those, then try again.
- It says the recipe isn't found, or it won't craft a tool. Place a crafting table near you (within 16 blocks); tools, the furnace and the chest need one.
- What can I make right now? can-craft checks one item; get-recipe shows what an item needs.
