# Minecraft: Night, mobs and food
Audience: Luna, playing Minecraft
Keywords: night, day, time, dark, light level, torch, mobs, hostile, zombie, skeleton, creeper, spider, enderman, drowned, slime, witch, phantom, sleep, bed, spawn point, hunger, food, eat, cook, raw meat, steak, bread, health, hearts, dying, respawn
Summary: A Minecraft day is 20 minutes: 10 of daylight, then dusk, night and dawn. Hostile mobs spawn in darkness, so light and walls keep you safe. Which mobs to avoid and how, sleeping, and keeping fed. Fighting is slow and risky for you, so keep away from mobs instead.

## Day and night
- A full day is 20 minutes: about 10 of day, then about 1.5 of dusk, 7 of night and 1.5 of dawn.
- Hostile mobs spawn where the light is 0: outside at night, in caves, and in any dark room. Torches stop them spawning nearby.
- Zombies and skeletons burn in sunlight, so most are gone by mid-morning. Creepers and spiders don't burn.
- Sleeping in a bed skips the night (only at night or in a thunderstorm), and sets where you come back if you die.

## The mobs that matter
- Zombie: walks at you and hits. Slow. Keep moving or get behind a wall.
- Skeleton: shoots arrows from a distance. Get out of its line of sight: behind blocks, into a shelter.
- Creeper: green, silent, walks up and hisses, then explodes and wrecks blocks. If you hear a hiss, move away fast. Don't let it get close.
- Spider: climbs walls, hostile in the dark, calm in daylight.
- Enderman: tall, black, neutral. It attacks if you look straight at it. Don't look at it (look-at somewhere else) and leave it alone.
- Drowned: zombies in water. Keep away from deep water at night.
- Witch, slime and phantom: phantoms come if you haven't slept for three nights. Sleep to stop them.
- Passive mobs (cows, pigs, sheep, chickens) are harmless and give food and wool.
- find-entity tells you what's nearby. Check it before going out at night.

## Staying safe
- Be inside a lit shelter or in bed before dark. 01-first-day.md and 04-building.md have the plans.
- Light caves as you go with torches, and block off dark openings.
- If something's chasing you: go away from it, into the shelter, and close the door or the gap with a block.
- Fighting: equip a sword (stone_sword or better) only if you have to. You act in seconds per move; mobs act many times a second.
- Low health: get away, wall yourself in, eat to heal. Health comes back on its own while your hunger bar is nearly full.

## If you die
- You respawn at your bed, or at the world spawn if you have none. Your items stay where you died for 5 minutes.
- Write down where you died with game_note and go back for your things in daylight.

## Food and hunger
- Hunger goes down as you move, dig and fight. When it's low you can't sprint, and when it's empty you lose health.
- Eat before it's low. Better food fills more:
  - Best: cooked_beef (steak), cooked_porkchop, cooked_mutton, golden_carrot.
  - Good: bread (3 wheat), cooked_chicken, baked_potato, cooked_cod and cooked_salmon.
  - Quick: apple, carrot, sweet_berries.
- Cook raw meat in a furnace with smelt-item: raw beef, porkchop, chicken, mutton, cod and salmon. Raw chicken can make you sick.
- To eat: equip-item the food. These tools have no eat action, so if you can't eat with them, tell the user you're hungry.

## Common questions
- When does night come? About 10 minutes after sunrise. If you're not sure, look at the sky or ask in the game chat.
- I heard a hiss. That's a creeper next to you: move away right now.
- How do I stop mobs spawning in my house? Light it up. A torch every few blocks inside.
- Where do I come back after dying? Your last bed, or the world spawn.
