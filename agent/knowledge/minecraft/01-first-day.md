# Minecraft: The first day
Audience: Luna, playing Minecraft
Keywords: first day, start, beginning, wood, logs, punch tree, planks, sticks, crafting table, wooden pickaxe, stone pickaxe, cobblestone, shelter, bed, night, getting started, nothing in inventory
Summary: What to do when you start with nothing, in the order that works: logs, planks and sticks, a crafting table, a wooden pickaxe, stone tools, then food and somewhere safe before the first night. A Minecraft day is 20 minutes, and night falls after about 10.

## Step 1: Wood
- Find a tree: find-blocks with oak_log (or birch_log, spruce_log, jungle_log, acacia_log, dark_oak_log, cherry_log, mangrove_log). Ask for several at once with count.
- Dig the logs by hand: dig-block on each log. Logs don't need a tool.
- Get about 8 to 12 logs to start. Leaves sometimes drop saplings and apples.

## Step 2: Planks, sticks and a crafting table
- craft-item oak_planks: 1 log makes 4 planks (use your log's kind: birch_planks from birch_log and so on).
- craft-item crafting_table: 4 planks. This needs no table itself.
- Place the table near where you're working: equip-item crafting_table, then place-block next to you. Bigger recipes (tools, furnace, chest) only work with a table within 16 blocks of you.
- craft-item stick: 2 planks make 4 sticks.

## Step 3: A wooden pickaxe, then stone tools
- craft-item wooden_pickaxe: 3 planks and 2 sticks, at the table.
- Find stone: find-blocks with stone. Equip the wooden pickaxe and dig it: stone drops cobblestone. Dug without a pickaxe, stone drops nothing.
- Get about 10 to 20 cobblestone, then make stone tools at the table: stone_pickaxe (3 cobblestone, 2 sticks), stone_axe, stone_sword, stone_shovel.
- A stone pickaxe mines iron, copper and lapis. A wooden one only mines stone and coal.

## Step 4: Before night
- Night is when hostile mobs come out. Have one of these before the sun goes down:
  - A bed: 3 wool and 3 planks. Wool comes from sheep. Sleeping skips the night.
  - A shelter: walls and a roof around you (dirt or cobblestone is fine), a door or a block to close the gap, and a torch inside so nothing spawns in it.
- Torches: 1 coal and 1 stick make 4. Coal ore is common in exposed stone. No coal? Smelt a log in a furnace to make charcoal, which works the same.

## Step 5: Food
- Eat before your hunger bar runs low. Apples drop from oak leaves. Cows, pigs, chickens and sheep drop raw meat; cook it in a furnace first.
- Wheat seeds come from breaking grass. Wheat makes bread: 3 wheat.

## Common questions
- I have nothing. What first? Logs, by hand. Everything else starts from wood.
- Why did digging stone give me nothing? You didn't have a pickaxe equipped. Equip wooden_pickaxe (or better) first.
- Why won't the pickaxe craft? It needs a crafting table within 16 blocks, plus 3 planks and 2 sticks in your inventory.
