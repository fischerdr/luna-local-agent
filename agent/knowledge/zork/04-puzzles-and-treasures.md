# Zork I: Puzzles and treasures (SPOILERS)
Audience: Luna, playing Zork I
Spoilers: yes
Keywords: Zork, spoilers, solution, walkthrough, hints, troll, cyclops, thief, dam, reservoir, boat, Loud Room, echo, exorcism, bell book candles, rainbow, sceptre, coal mine, diamond, egg, canary, trophy case, score, treasures
Summary: How the main puzzles in Zork I are solved, and the list of treasures to bring back to the trophy case. These are spoilers: try first, and look here when you're properly stuck. This file is general knowledge about the game, not from the scanned map.

## Getting in and getting light
- Open the window Behind House and go in through the Kitchen.
- The lamp is in the Living Room. Most of the underground is dark, and in the dark a grue can kill you. The lamp's battery doesn't last forever, so turn it off when you don't need it.
- Move the rug in the Living Room to find the trap door, open it, and go down to the Cellar. It usually shuts behind you.

## The troll
- The troll blocks the Troll Room. Fight him with the sword from the Living Room (it glows when enemies are near). It can take a few blows, and it's safer to save the game before.

## The cyclops
- Say "Ulysses" (or "Odysseus") in the Cyclops Room. The cyclops runs off, smashing the wall open, which joins the Strange Passage to the Living Room: a quick way home.

## The thief
- A thief wanders the underground and steals things, treasures especially. His lair is the Treasure Room above the Cyclops Room, where whatever he took ends up.
- The jewel-encrusted egg is very hard to open without breaking it. Let the thief take it: he opens it, and you get it back from his lair once you beat him.
- Beat him when you're stronger, later in the game. A good weapon helps, and the nasty knife from the Attic works.

## The dam, the reservoir and the Loud Room
- In the Maintenance Room, push the yellow button (a green bubble on the dam lights up).
- At the Dam, turn the bolt with the wrench. The sluice gates open, the reservoir drains, and you can walk across to the trunk of jewels and on to Reservoir North.
- In the Loud Room, say "echo". The room goes quiet and you can take the platinum bar.

## The boat and the river
- The pile of plastic at the Dam Base is a boat. Inflate it with the air pump from Reservoir North.
- Don't carry anything sharp into it: the knife or sword can puncture it.
- Launch, ride downstream and land on the east bank at the Sandy Beach or the Shore before Aragain Falls.
- The buoy in Frigid River (4) has an emerald in it.
- Dig in the Sandy Cave with the shovel (several times) for the scarab.

## The rainbow
- Wave the sceptre (in the gold coffin) at Aragain Falls or at the End of Rainbow. The rainbow turns solid: walk across and find the pot of gold at the End of Rainbow.

## The temple and Hades
- Tie the rope from the Attic to the railing in the Dome Room and climb down to the Torch Room.
- Take the brass bell from the Temple, and the candles and black book from the Altar. Bring a light for the candles: the matchbook from the Dam Lobby.
- At the Entrance to Hades: ring the bell, light the candles, read the black book. The spirits flee, and the crystal skull is in the Land of the Dead.
- The Altar's hole only lets you through carrying very little. Pray at the Altar to be sent back to the forest above ground.

## The mirrors
- Rub the mirror in either Mirror Room to swap to the other one: a shortcut between north and south.

## The coal mine and the diamond
- Garlic (in the brown sack from the Kitchen) keeps the vampire bat away. Without it the bat carries you off somewhere else.
- No open flame in the Gas Room (no lit torch, candles or matches): it explodes. The lamp is safe.
- Put the things you'll need in the basket in the Shaft Room and lower it. Through the coal mine and down the ladder, you can only squeeze into the Drafty Room carrying almost nothing, so pick them up from the basket there.
- Put coal in the machine in the Machine Room, close the lid, and turn the switch with the screwdriver. It makes a huge diamond. Send the diamond up in the basket.

## The canary
- Inside the egg is a golden clockwork canary. Wind it in the forest and a songbird drops a brass bauble.

## Treasures for the trophy case
Points come from finding each treasure, then more for putting it in the trophy case. The full score is 350.
- Jewel-encrusted egg (up the tree)
- Golden clockwork canary (inside the egg)
- Brass bauble (from winding the canary in the forest)
- Painting (Gallery)
- Platinum bar (Loud Room)
- Ivory torch (Torch Room)
- Gold coffin (Egyptian Room)
- Sceptre (inside the coffin)
- Trunk of jewels (Reservoir, once drained)
- Crystal trident (Atlantis Room)
- Jade figurine (Bat Room)
- Sapphire bracelet (Gas Room)
- Huge diamond (made in the Machine Room)
- Bag of coins (Maze 5)
- Crystal skull (Land of the Dead)
- Silver chalice (Treasure Room, the thief's lair)
- Pot of gold (End of Rainbow, once the rainbow is solid)
- Large emerald (inside the buoy)
- Jewelled scarab (dug up in the Sandy Cave)

## The ending
- With every treasure in the case, a map appears in it. It shows the way to the Stone Barrow southwest of the West of House, and going in ends Zork I (and leads on to Zork II).

## Common questions
- I'm in the dark and a grue might eat me. Leave the way you came, or turn on the lamp.
- The trap door shut behind me. Find another way up: the Studio chimney (carrying little), the Grating Room, or the Strange Passage once the cyclops has gone.
- I keep getting robbed. That's the thief. Your things end up in his lair above the Cyclops Room.
- I ran out of light. The lamp battery runs down; the torch and candles also give light. Save light by switching the lamp off above ground.
