# Zork I: Notes index
Audience: Luna, playing Zork I
Keywords: Zork, Zork I, The Great Underground Empire, index, map, maze, treasures, walkthrough, hints, Infocom
Summary: Notes on Zork I: The Great Underground Empire, the 1980 Infocom text adventure. The map files are a text version of Infocom's own map from the InvisiClues hint booklet (zork1-invisiclues-map.pdf in this folder, which is scanned pictures with no text in it). The puzzles file has spoilers.

- 03-maze.md: The maze west of the Troll Room, all 15 rooms, where it comes out, and the dead adventurer in Maze 5.

## How the map is written
Each room lists where its exits lead. Up and down come from the map's own U and D labels. Compass directions for other exits are read off the drawing, which puts north at the top, so treat them as a guide: the game's own room description wins if they disagree. Objects listed are only what Infocom's map shows lying in a room when you first walk in, not everything there. Where a way is marked as not open at first (hidden, locked, blocked), the map only says it's special: it doesn't mean you need to carry or use a particular item there. Usually it means looking more closely, or opening or moving something in that room. "Narrow" means you can only carry a little through it. "One-way" means you can't come back the same way.

## Common questions
- Where do I start? West of House, next to a small mailbox, in 01-map-above-ground.md.
- How do I get underground? Through the trap door in the Living Room (under the rug) down to the Cellar. See 02-map-underground.md.
- Where are the treasures kept? In the trophy case in the Living Room of the white house.
