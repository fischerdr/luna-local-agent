# Zork I: Map above ground
Audience: Luna, playing Zork I
Keywords: Zork, map, West of House, white house, mailbox, forest, clearing, kitchen, living room, attic, canyon, rainbow, barrow, grating, tree, egg
Summary: The surface of Zork I: a white house in a forest, with a canyon and a rainbow to the east. You start West of House. The house is entered through the kitchen window at the back, and the way underground is through the Living Room. From Infocom's InvisiClues map.

## Around the house
- West of House (the start; a small mailbox here): north to North of House, south to South of House, west into Forest (1). Southwest leads to the Stone Barrow, but that path only opens once the game is won.
- North of House: north to the Forest Path, west to West of House, east to Behind House.
- South of House: west to West of House, east to Behind House, south into Forest (3).
- Behind House: the kitchen window is here and it starts closed (open it, then go west into the Kitchen). North to North of House, south to South of House, east to the Clearing (east).

## Inside the house
- Kitchen (glass bottle, brown sack): up to the Attic, west to the Living Room, east out of the window to Behind House. A narrow chimney comes up into the kitchen from the Studio underground (one-way, upwards only, and you can carry very little).
- Attic (rope, nasty knife): down to the Kitchen.
- Living Room (trophy case, lamp, sword): east to the Kitchen. Down to the Cellar through a trap door, but it's hidden at first: something in the room is covering it. West to the Strange Passage through a door that won't open from this side at first.

## The forest
- Forest (1): east to the Forest Path, northeast to the Clearing (north), and a path south toward Forest (3).
- Forest Path: north to the Clearing (north), up into the tree (Up a Tree), south to North of House, west to Forest (1), east to Forest (2).
- Up a Tree (jewel-encrusted egg, in a bird's nest): down to the Forest Path.
- Clearing (north) (pile of leaves): south to the Forest Path, southwest to Forest (1), southeast to Forest (2). Under the leaves is a grating down to the Grating Room, locked from below.
- Forest (2): west to the Forest Path, east to Forest (4), south to the Clearing (east).
- Forest (4): back to Forest (2); most ways just loop round.
- Forest (3): north to the Clearing (east), northwest to South of House, west toward Forest (1).

## East: the canyon and the rainbow
- Clearing (east): west to Behind House, north to Forest (2), south to Forest (3), southeast to Canyon View.
- Canyon View: down to the Rocky Ledge. A path west drops back to Forest (3) (one-way).
- Rocky Ledge: up to Canyon View, down to the Canyon Bottom.
- Canyon Bottom: up to the Rocky Ledge, north to the End of Rainbow.
- End of Rainbow: back to the Canyon Bottom. The rainbow (On the Rainbow) can't be walked on at first.

## The barrow
- Stone Barrow: northeast to West of House. West into the barrow (Inside the Barrow), which is the way on to Zork II. It only opens after Zork I is won.

## Common questions
- How do I get into the house? Go around to Behind House, open the window, and go west into the Kitchen.
- Where's the lamp? In the Living Room. Take it before going underground, where most places are dark.
- Where's the egg? Up the tree from the Forest Path.
- What's under the leaves in the north clearing? A grating down to the Grating Room, which is locked from below.
