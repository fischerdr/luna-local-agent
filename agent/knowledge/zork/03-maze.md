# Zork I: The maze
Audience: Luna, playing Zork I
Keywords: Zork, maze, twisty little passages, all alike, troll room, cyclops room, grating room, skeleton, skeleton key, bag of coins, rusty knife, useless lantern, dead end
Summary: The maze west of the Troll Room in Zork I has 15 rooms that all look alike. It comes out in three places: the Troll Room (from Maze 1), the Grating Room (from Maze 11) and the Cyclops Room (from Maze 15). Maze 5 has a dead adventurer with a skeleton key and a bag of coins. From Infocom's InvisiClues map.

## Before going in
- Maze (every one of its rooms is called just "Maze"): the ways out are the Troll Room from Maze 1, the Grating Room from Maze 11 and the Cyclops Room from Maze 15. The skeleton key and the bag of coins are in Maze 5. Drop something in each room to tell them apart.

The map numbers the rooms but doesn't give compass directions for most passages, only up and down where it shows them. Every room has the same description, so drop a worthless item in a room to tell it apart, and write down with game_note which exit led where. Several passages are one-way, and some rooms have an exit that loops back to the same room.

## Rooms and connections
- Maze 1: to the Troll Room (east), to Maze 4, to Maze 2 (a long passage), and one exit loops back to Maze 1.
- Maze 2: to Maze 3 (east), to Maze 1. Down leads to Maze 4 (one-way).
- Maze 3: to Maze 2 (west), to Maze 4. Up leads to Maze 5.
- Maze 4: to Maze 1, to Maze 3, to a dead end.
- Maze 5 (useless lantern, skeleton key, bag of coins, rusty knife, skeleton): to Maze 3, to Maze 6, to a dead end (east). Maze 12 has a one-way passage in here.
- Maze 6: to Maze 5, to Maze 7 (east), to Maze 9 (up), and one exit loops back to Maze 6.
- Maze 7: to Maze 6 (west), to Maze 8, to Maze 15, up to Maze 14. Down leads to a dead end (one-way).
- Maze 8: to Maze 7, to a dead end, and one exit loops back to Maze 8.
- Maze 9: to Maze 6, to Maze 10, to Maze 12, to Maze 13. Down leads to Maze 11 (one-way). One exit loops back to Maze 9.
- Maze 10: to Maze 9, to Maze 13, up to Maze 11.
- Maze 11: to the Grating Room, to Maze 10, to Maze 12, to Maze 13. Maze 9 has a one-way passage in here.
- Maze 12: to Maze 13 (east), to a dead end (north), to Maze 11, up to Maze 9. Down leads to Maze 5 (one-way).
- Maze 13: to Maze 9, to Maze 10, to Maze 11, to Maze 12.
- Maze 14: to Maze 7, to Maze 15, and one exit loops back to Maze 14.
- Maze 15: to Maze 7, to Maze 14, to the Cyclops Room (southeast).

## Useful routes (by room)
- Troll Room to the skeleton: Troll Room, Maze 1, Maze 4, Maze 3, then up to Maze 5.
- Skeleton to the Cyclops Room: Maze 5, Maze 6, Maze 7, Maze 15, then the Cyclops Room.
- Skeleton to the Grating Room: Maze 5, Maze 6, up to Maze 9, down to Maze 11, then the Grating Room. The skeleton key unlocks the grating from below.

## Common questions
- How do I tell the rooms apart? Drop something you don't need in each one and note which.
- What's worth having in the maze? The skeleton key (it opens the grating) and the bag of coins (a treasure) in Maze 5. The rusty knife and the useless lantern are no good.
- Where does the maze come out? Troll Room (Maze 1), Grating Room (Maze 11), Cyclops Room (Maze 15).
