# Zork I: Map underground (The Great Underground Empire)
Audience: Luna, playing Zork I
Keywords: Zork, underground, cellar, troll room, round room, loud room, dam, reservoir, Frigid River, temple, altar, Hades, Land of the Dead, mirror room, coal mine, bat room, gas room, cyclops, treasure room, studio, gallery, Atlantis
Summary: Everything below the white house in Zork I, from Infocom's InvisiClues map. The Cellar is under the Living Room. The Troll Room north of the Cellar leads west into the maze and east to the Round Room, the hub of the underground. Beyond it are the dam and reservoir, the Frigid River, the temple and Hades, and the coal mine in the far north.

## Under the house
- Cellar: north to the Troll Room, south to East of Chasm, up to the Living Room through the trap door (it tends to be shut above you once you're down). The slide from the Slide Room lands here (one-way).
- East of Chasm: north to the Cellar, east to the Gallery.
- Gallery (painting): west to East of Chasm, north to the Studio.
- Studio (piece of paper): south to the Gallery. Up the chimney to the Kitchen (narrow: you can carry very little).
- Troll Room: south to the Cellar, west into the maze (see 03-maze.md), east to the East-West Passage. A troll blocks the way.

## The Round Room and around it
- East-West Passage: west to the Troll Room, east to the Round Room, north to the Chasm.
- Round Room (the hub): west to the East-West Passage, north to the North-South Passage, east to the Loud Room, south to the Narrow Passage, southeast to the Engravings Cave.
- North-South Passage: south to the Round Room, north to the Chasm, northeast to the Deep Canyon.
- Chasm: south to the North-South Passage, southwest to the East-West Passage, northeast to Reservoir South.
- Deep Canyon: down to the Loud Room, southwest to the North-South Passage, northwest to Reservoir South.
- Loud Room (platinum bar): west to the Round Room, up to the Deep Canyon, east to the Damp Cave. It's deafening in there.
- Damp Cave: west to the Loud Room, east to White Cliffs Beach (North).

## The dam and the reservoir
- Reservoir South: west to the Stream View, east to the Dam, southwest to the Chasm, southeast to the Deep Canyon. North across the reservoir to the Reservoir, once the water's out of the way.
- Stream View: east to Reservoir South. The Stream beyond can't be reached on foot.
- Reservoir (trunk of jewels): south to Reservoir South, north to Reservoir North, west to the Stream (not on foot).
- Reservoir North (air pump): south back across the reservoir, north to the Atlantis Room.
- Atlantis Room (crystal trident): south to Reservoir North, up to the Cave (north).
- Dam: north to the Dam Lobby, west to Reservoir South, down to the Dam Base.
- Dam Lobby (matchbook, guidebook): south to the Dam, north to the Maintenance Room.
- Maintenance Room (tool chest, tube, screwdriver, wrench): back to the Dam Lobby. There are coloured buttons here.
- Dam Base (pile of plastic): up to the Dam. Onto the Frigid River only by boat.

## The Frigid River
- The river runs south in five stretches, Frigid River (1) to (5), and going between them needs a boat.
- Frigid River (1): from the Dam Base.
- Frigid River (3): White Cliffs Beach (North) is on its west bank.
- Frigid River (4) (buoy): White Cliffs Beach (South) is on its west bank and Sandy Beach on its east bank.
- Frigid River (5): the Shore is on its east bank. Past it the river goes over Aragain Falls: don't stay on it.
- White Cliffs Beach (North): west to the Damp Cave, south to White Cliffs Beach (South).
- White Cliffs Beach (South): north to White Cliffs Beach (North).
- Sandy Beach (shovel): northeast to the Sandy Cave, south to the Shore.
- Sandy Cave: back to the Sandy Beach.
- Shore: north to the Sandy Beach, south to Aragain Falls.
- Aragain Falls: north to the Shore. West onto the rainbow (On the Rainbow), but it can't be walked on at first.
- On the Rainbow: east to Aragain Falls, west to the End of Rainbow (above ground).

## South: the temple and Hades
- Engravings Cave: northwest to the Round Room, east to the Dome Room.
- Dome Room: west to the Engravings Cave. Down to the Torch Room is a long drop: no way down at first.
- Torch Room (torch): south to the Temple.
- Temple (brass bell): north to the Torch Room, east to the Egyptian Room, south to the Altar.
- Egyptian Room (gold coffin): west to the Temple.
- Altar (candles, black book): north to the Temple. A narrow hole leads down to the Cave (south) (one-way and narrow).
- Narrow Passage: north to the Round Room, south to the Mirror Room (South).
- Mirror Room (South): north to the Narrow Passage, west to the Winding Passage, south to the Cave (south). There's a big mirror here.
- Winding Passage: north to the Mirror Room (South), east to the Cave (south).
- Cave (south): north to the Mirror Room (South), west to the Winding Passage, down to the Entrance to Hades.
- Entrance to Hades: up to the Cave (south). South into the Land of the Dead, but spirits bar the way.
- Land of the Dead (crystal skull): back north to the Entrance to Hades.

## West: the cyclops
- Cyclops Room: northwest into the maze (Maze 15). East to the Strange Passage, but the cyclops is in the way. Up to the Treasure Room.
- Treasure Room (silver chalice): down to the Cyclops Room. It's the thief's lair.
- Strange Passage: west to the Cyclops Room, east to the Living Room (through the door that starts shut).
- Grating Room: southwest into the maze (Maze 11). Up through the grating to the Clearing (north), but it's locked.

## North: the mirror room, the mine and the bats
- Slide Room: east to the Cold Passage, north to the Mine Entrance, down the slide to the Cellar (one-way).
- Cold Passage: west to the Slide Room, south to the Mirror Room (North).
- Mirror Room (North): north to the Cold Passage, west to the Twisting Passage, south to the Cave (north). It has a big mirror like the southern one.
- Twisting Passage: north to the Mirror Room (North), east to the Cave (north).
- Cave (north): north to the Mirror Room (North), west to the Twisting Passage. It also connects up and down with the Atlantis Room.
- Mine Entrance: south to the Slide Room, west to the Squeaky Room.
- Squeaky Room: east to the Mine Entrance. North to the Bat Room (a bat lives there).
- Bat Room (jade figurine): south to the Squeaky Room, east to the Shaft Room. There's a vampire bat.
- Shaft Room (basket): west to the Bat Room, north to the Smelly Room. The basket hangs on a chain down the shaft.
- Smelly Room: south to the Shaft Room, down to the Gas Room.
- Gas Room (sapphire bracelet): up to the Smelly Room, east to Coal Mine (1). It smells of gas: no open flame.
- Coal Mine (1) to (4): a small maze of four rooms, several of which loop back on themselves. Coal Mine (4) leads down to the Ladder Top.
- Ladder Top: up to Coal Mine (4), down to the Ladder Bottom.
- Ladder Bottom: up to the Ladder Top, south to a Dead End (coal), west to the Timber Room.
- Timber Room (timber): east to the Ladder Bottom. West to the Drafty Room is narrow: you can carry almost nothing.
- Drafty Room: east to the Timber Room (narrow), south to the Machine Room. The basket from the Shaft Room comes down here.
- Machine Room: north to the Drafty Room. There's a machine here.

## Common questions
- How do I get past the troll? He has to be fought. See 04-puzzles-and-treasures.md.
- Why is the Loud Room so loud? Water from the dam. See 04-puzzles-and-treasures.md for how to quieten it.
- How do I cross the reservoir? It has to be drained at the dam first.
- How do I get onto the river? You need a boat: the pile of plastic at the Dam Base.
- Which way back to the surface if the trap door is shut? Up the chimney from the Studio (carrying little), or through the Strange Passage once the cyclops has gone, or out through the Grating Room.
